**Length:** ~90 seconds

*(pause)*

"Luckily... ippudu aa time dorukuthundhi."

"Andhuke badminton coaching thisukovadam start chesa."

"He is my trainer... peru Vinay" 🙂

"i'm getting better than before. Day by day. "

- Coach Vinay feeding shots / correcting form — shows the real training

- Sandy missing a shot and laughing — keep it real, don't hide the mistakes
- Sandy landing a good shot — the satisfaction moment
- End on Sandy walking off court, relaxed and happy
