# 10K — I Called It Before I Even Started

**Format:** Reel
**Length:** ~90–120 seconds
**Tone:** Excited, energetic — not sentimental, not a speech, just real excitement

---

## HOOK
*(Play the old clip / show on screen)*

"Ee video nenu Instagram start chese  mundhu roju record chesanu... It was a dream for me."

"And now we've reached 10k followers... but 1yr lo kadhu... 8 months lo."

---

## INTRO
*(Warm, welcoming — for new viewers)*

Miru nannu first time chusthunte, Hi... this is Sandeep Bachala and friends call me Sandy.

US nunchi India ki move avuthunna time lo ,
naa life ni document cheyyadaniki and naku thelisina information share cheyyadaniki ee channel start chesanu.

---

## THE JOURNEY
*(Honest, real)*

Ee channel start chesetappudu  nakoka plan anedhi em ledhu...ela thisukuvelthano kuda thelidhu..

Just Naa story cheppali ane start chesa.

kani, "Ni videos chusthunte, nannu chusthunnate undhi, ani naku vacche comments ki, i was so overwhelmed"

---

## THE NUMBER
*(Genuine, not dramatic)*

10k.... vinadaniki it might sound like a small number...

But 10 thousand people daily life lo busy ga untu, naa videos chusthunnarante... it means a lot for me. Thank you so much to everyone.

---

## THE LESSON
*(Calm, sincere)*

Naa peddha dream ni achieve cheyyadaniki ee 10k is a milestone

---

## ENDING

And there are so many videos that i am planning... and you guys gonna love it... so inka follow avvakapothe follow button kotti pettandi.

"See you all in the next one." 🙂
