# Is Adjusting Really Saving You Money?

**Format:** Reel
**Length:** ~90 seconds
**Tone:** Personal, honest, slightly provocative — real talk from experience, not a lecture

---

## HOOK
*(Direct to camera)*

"Manalo chala mandhi edhaina manaki nacchindhi kondham anukoni..."

*(pause)*

"Akkada 20 dollars oo, 30 dollars oo items ki kuda..."

"Endhukule... ippudu avasarama ani aagipothuntam."

*(pause)*

"Nenu kuda alane undevadini."

## THE PS4 STORY
*(Personal, relatable)*

"Naku entho istamaina PS4 kondham ani chusthu..."

"Endhuku le... 400 dollars pettadam dhaniki ani..."

*(pause)*

"Chala sarlu aagipoya."

*(Storytelling, building)*

"Okasari emaindante..."

"Badminton aaduthu... kindha padi hand twist ayyindhi."

*(pause)*

"Maa manager fracture ayyinattu undhi... chusuko ani bayapedithe..."

"Parigetthukuntu emergency ki vellipoya."

*(pause)*

"Akkada vadu... fracture ledhu thokka ledhu ani..."

"Oka ointment icchi..."

*(beat)*

"1500 dollar bill yesadu."

*(pause — let it hit)*

"Aa time lo naku anipinchindhi okate..."

"Nenu naa istalanni aapukoni... save chesukunna money mottham..."

"Thisukelli... anavaramga veediki posane ani."

## THE REALIZATION
*(Calm, genuine — this is the turn)*

"Oka 2 days ade frustration lo unna..."

*(pause)*

"Kani aa moment naa life ni marchesindhi."

*(pause)*

"Aa time lo naku ardham ayyindhi..."

"Manam aalochinchalsindhi Mana istalanni aapukoni... ila chinna chinna vatiki ela save cheyyali ani kadhu..."

*(pause)*

"Mana needs ni matrame kakunda, Manaki wants ni kuda satisfy chesukovalante..."

"Entha kastapadali... and ela antha earn cheyyali ani."

---

## THE TRUTH
*(Direct, slightly provocative)*

"Mana ego hurt ayyela okati cheptha..."

*(pause)*

"Mana wants ni satisfy chesukunela money earn cheyyadam..."

"Chala easy."

*(pause)*

"Kani mana laziness valla..."

"Vere options ni explore cheyyakunda..."

"Manaki vacche salary lo adjust ayipothu gadipesthuntam."

*(pause — firm)*

"Trust me... adhi peaceful ga brathakadam kadhu."

"Its a curse."

"Manam save chesune money eppudu pothundho, evvaram cheppalem"

## THE MESSAGE
*(Warm, direct)*

"So nenu cheppedhi entante..."

"Explore the options around you."

*(pause)*

"Give the best to your family..."

"And to yourself."
