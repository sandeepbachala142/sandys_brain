# I Lost My Dream Job

**Format:** Reel
**Length:** ~90–120 seconds
**Tone:** Raw, honest, personal — not motivational speaker, just real

---

## HOOK
*(Direct to camera — calm, serious)*

"Career lo downfall lo unna, or Life chala kastam ga anipisthunna valla kosam ee video"

---

## THE PEAK
"US lo unnappudu nenu naa dream job crack chesa."
"180k dollars PA... 80k dollars ki stocks."
"Naa career lo ne nenu crack chesina best job."

"inka job ae life ani anukuntu, India ki kuda adhe company tho move ayipovacchu ani future mottham plan chesesa."

"project antha nenu handle chesevadini kabatti...Maa manager ayithe thega prema chupinchevadu"

---

## THE CALL
Life chala bagundedhi.

"Aa time lo family india ki visa stamping ki vaccharu."

"Oka week lo nenu vellali."

"And then... oka call vacchindhi." 

"konni situations valla Ni job layoff ayyindhi ani. join ayyi Year kuda avvaledhu ani 80k stocks kuda ivvaledhu.

"Nenu ventane maa manager ki call chesanu... Phone lift cheyyaledhu."
"Enno sarlu Message chesa... No response."
"I was so desparate"

"idhi naa company anukuntu, ratri pagalu, pani chesthe, nakicche respect idha anipinchidhi"

"Naa career lo ne top lo unna nenu..."

"Okkasariga mottham collapse ayipoya."

---

## THE TRAP

"Family emo india lo irukkupoyindhi..."

"Nenu akkada okkadine undevadini"

"Dream job kadha... so konni days it haunted me."

---

## THE SHIFT

"Appati dhaka side income ki edho ala ala try chesthunna naku... aa situation oka peddha boost icchindhi."

"IT job permanent kadhu ani, naa business options midhe, naa life ae depend ayinattu work chesa."

"And guess what..."

"Oka 9 months tarvatha..."

"naa patha manager ki oka message petta."

**"Thank you so much... for not picking up my call."**

---

## THE MESSAGE
*(Warm, genuine — not preachy)*

"Aa confidence ae nenu IT career ni vadhileyyadaniki... and completely india ki move avvadaniki help chesindhi."
and now "I am living my life ... living in my own terms"

Remember "Manam life lo entha suffer ayyam  annadhi  evvaru pattinchukoru..."

"Dhani nunchi entha gattiga bounce back ayyam annadi will tell you what you are."

And Intha varaku miru naa video chusthunte, follow button kottandi.

---

## ENDING


"See you all in the next one." 🙂
