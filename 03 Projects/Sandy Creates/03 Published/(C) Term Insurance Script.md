# Term Insurance

**Format:** Reel
**Length:** ~90 seconds
**Tone:** Honest, genuine, not preachy — real talk from a friend

---

## HOOK
*(Direct to camera, calm and confident)*

"Next 10 seconds nenu cheppindhi vinna tarvatha..."

*(pause)*

"Mi family safe ani anipisthe..."

"Ee video skip chesi velipondi."

*(pause)*

"Meeru ippudu lekapoyithe..."

"Mee family ki or mee parents ki..."

"Repu morning nunchi income vasthundha?"

*(pause)*

"Vaallu enni years survive avvadaniki mi dhaggara savings unnay?"

*(pause)*

"Ee rendu questions ki confident ga answer cheyagaligithe... your family is in safe zone."

*(pause)*

"Cheyalekapothe... mee kosam ee video." 🎯

---

## THE REVEAL
*(Pause — text on screen: TERM INSURANCE)*

"TERM INSURANCE."

*(pause)*

"Ee word ni miru inthaka mundhu vini, neglect chesunte..."

"Ee video mimmalni vethukkuntu vacchindhi ani nammandi."

---

## WHAT IS TERM INSURANCE
*(Simple, clear — not technical)*

"Term insurance anedhi oka vyakti ki emaina ayithe..."

"Aa family ki  vacche oka bulk amount."

*(pause)*

"Its not just a regular insurance..."

"Adhi mana family ki oka Raksha kavacham lantidhi."

*(pause)*

"Manaki emaina ayithe..."

"Mana family ki max one or 2 years matrame emotional ga effect untundhi... and then they must move on"

"But financial burden matram life long undipothundhi."

---

## THE REALITY — 7 OUT OF 10
*(Personal, shocked)*

Ee video cheyyadaniki main reason enti ante, 
"Niku term insurance undha? ane question ni naa friends ni adigithe..."

*(pause)*

"7 out of 10 members cheppina answer... NO."

*(pause — genuine shock)*

"I was seriously shocked."

---

## THE EXCUSES PEOPLE MAKE
*(Warm, not judging)*

"Endhuku negative ga matladathav?"

"Manakem avuthundhi?"

*(pause)*

"Ilanti matala kanna..."

"Mana family ni safety net lo unchadam mana main priority."

---

## THE CALL TO ACTION
*(Urgent but warm)*

"Ippati miku term insurance lekapothe..."

"Okka sec kuda aalochinchakunda ventane thisukoni."

*(pause)*

"India lo unde vallaki or US lo undevallaki..."

"Term insurance thisukovali ante..."

"Details kosam TERM ani comment cheyyandi."

"Details miku DM chestha."

*(pause — soft smile)*

"Idhi nenu evariki promotion cheyyadam ledhu..."

"I genuinely want to help."

---

## ENDING

"Hope andhariki help ayyindhi anukuntunnanu."

"See you all in the next one." 🙂

- **"7 out of 10"** — text overlay on screen, visual stat
- **"TERM"** — show comment graphic at CTA moment
- Keep cuts minimal — this video works on words and delivery, not visuals
