# Digital India

**Format:** Reel
**Length:** ~90 seconds
**Tone:** Genuine, positive, eye-opening — not patriotic, just real

---

## HOOK
*(Direct to camera)*

"US nunchi india ki vasthunnappudu, konni vinnanu..."

*(pause)*

"US lo govt system easy ga untundhi, anni systematic lo ayipothay..."

"India lo ayithe government office la chuttu thiruguthu undali ani..."

*(pause)*

"Correct ae... may be 5-6 yrs mundhu dhaka adhe situation emo..."

"But ippudu..."

---

## THE EVIDENCE

*(Each point quick, personal, specific)*

**Aadhaar + PAN:**

"Aadhar digital, Pan card digital..."

"Naa patha aadhar nunchi kottha card with biometrics change cheyyali ante..."

"Mee seva ki velthe 15mins... photo lu, biometric lu thisi pani ayipoyindhi."

"Kottha card ragane, address change ayithe online lo ne ayipoyindhi."

*(pause)*

"Pan card nadhi 10yrs back dhi... kotthadhi apply cheyyali ante..."

"Ekkadikekkadiko vellanavasaram ledhu..."

"Just online lo 600 rupees tho 10mins lo ayipoyindhi."

**Bank Account:**

**SIM + Internet:**



"Home internet tho ayithe enni OTT lu free ga icchesthunnaro..."

"Netflix, Amazon prime, Hotstar, Zee5... You name it we have it annattu isthunnaru."

*(pause)*

"Yaa... konni companies through velle works slow unnay gani... but it will never be perfect kadha"

## THE TRUTH
*(Calm, genuine)*

"Govt  mottham digital chesina tarvatha..."

"Its amazing to see how things are turning out."

*(pause)*

"Evaraina inka govt through panulu alage late avuthu untay ani adhe thought lo unte..."

"Trust me... Its not like before."

*(pause)*

"Its Digital India now."
