# Complete Shipment from USA to India

**Format:** Reel / Short Vlog
**Length:** ~2 minutes
**Tone:** Informative, personal, honest — real experience, not a tutorial

---

## HOOK
*(Direct to camera)*

US nunchi india ki shipping chesina process ni ee video lo complete ga explain chesthanu.

"Cost nunchi customs varaku... anni."

---

## THE COMPANY
*(Matter of fact)*

"US lo konni companies ni research chesina tarvatha..."

"Universal Relocations ane company ki fix ayyam."

*(pause)*

"Vallu packing and shipping rendu handle chestharu."

ofcourse packing ki extra charge untundhi... 
"Renditiki kalipi pay chesam."

---

## WHAT WE SHIPPED
*(Animated, listing it out)*

"2 peddha TVs... 2 monitors... speakers..."

"Air purifiers... vacuum cleaners..."

"Inko chala items..."

*(pause)*

"Mottham kalipi... 32 boxes vacchay."

---

## THE COST
*(Honest, no sugarcoating)*

"Mottham kalipi naku around 4700 dollars ayyindhi."

*(pause)*

"Dhantlone vallu vacchi mottham pack chesandhuku Oka 500 dollars extra kuda add ayi undhi."

"budget lo veldham ante adhi thiseyocchu..."

---

## THE TIMELINE
*(Calm, informative)*

"Shipment US lo February starting lo vesthe Naku June lo vacchindhi."

"Usually inka fast ga vasthundhi..."

"Kani ee war situations valla koncham late ayyindhi."

---

## CUSTOMS — THE SURPRISE
*(Slightly relieved)*

"Chennai port ki vacchaka..."

"Inni items ki naku padina customs charges..."

*(pause for effect)*

"Just 20,000 rupees."

Yes miru vinnadhi correct ae... 

"Chala chala thakkuva."

"Enti antha thakkuva ani adigithe..."

"Oka rule undhanta..."

"Manam thecchukune electronics lo... every 1 unique item ki charge padadhu ."

so nenu 2 TV thesthe, 1 TV ki ... speakers lo 1 speaker ki charges emi padaledhu... 

"So most of the items ki naku charges padaledhu."

"Just oka kottha TV and speakers ki matrame customs charge chesaru."

---

## PORT & STORAGE CHARGES
*(Practical, informative)*

"Port charges avi ivi ani inko 8,000 rupees."

"Ikkadaki vacchaka... nenu ventane thisukoledhu."

"Storage kuda avasaram ayyindhi."

*(chinna pause)*

"Storage ki 1 month ki around 4,500 rupees."

"And one time loading and unloading ki inko 4,000 charge chesaru."

*(pause — important warning)*

"Ikkada oka peddha vishayam entante..."

"90 days kanna ekkuva store chesthe..."

"Mana total declared amount ki every month 9% charge chestharu."

*(pause)*

"Chala ekkuva. Jagrattha."

---

## DELIVERY & DAMAGE
*(Personal, honest)*

"Items mostly bagane vacchay..."

"Kani konni items ayithe damage ayyay."

*(chinna pause — genuine)*

"Luckily anni chinna items ae damage ayyay..."

*(pause — emotional, brief)*

"Kani chinnavi ayina... poyinavi just items kadhu..."

"Avi memories..."






"Koncham badhesindhi."

*(pause)*

"But em chestham."

---

## ENDING
*(Warm, helpful)*

"Hopefully shipping gurinchi mottham clarity icchanane anukuntunna."

*(pause)*

"Emaina miss ayithe..."

"Comment section lo cheppandi."

"See you all in the next one." 🙂
