Mistake number 2: 
"Prepare for the traffic."

*(pause)*

"Entho mandhi zero civic sense unde vallu road midha thiruguthuntaru..."

"US lo ayithe left and right chusthe saripoyedhi..."

"Ikkada ayithe... 360 degrees chusi drive cheyyali."

*(pause)*

"Trust me... its life changing and So peaceful."

*(pause — direct advice)*

"And tier 1 cities lo... only automatic gear car ae drive cheyyadaniki prefer cheyyandi."

*(beat)*

"Porapatuna manual thisukunnara..."

## RENTAL PROPERTY
*(For NRIs who rented out their Indian home)*

Mistake number 4:

"Idhi US lo illu rent ki icchi vacche vallaki..."

*(pause)*

"Mana illu ayithe, rent icchina 2 months ke naku dhimma thirige shock iccharu.."

"Tenants vere state ki jump anta."

*(pause)*

"Nenu chusukunna rental management company vadu... he is not licensed."

*(pause)*

"So vallu velipothunna... just advance apadam thappa... vadu em cheyyalekapoyadu."
