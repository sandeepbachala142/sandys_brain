# Trump Pampesada?

**Tone:** Funny, sharp, real — NRI experience

---

## HOOK

"Trump pampesada? Ayyo trump pampinchada?"

"Tumpuu pampesaadaa?"

---

## THE SPLIT

"Ikkada 2 types of people untaru —"

"Ayyo papam ani adigevallu 4% unte,"

"Adhoka paisachika aanandham pondhadaniki adige vallu 96% untaru."

"Dhani answer ippudu cheptha..."

---

## THE REAL ANSWER

"Most of the NRIs, konni personal issues valla and US lo unde limitations valla inni years ga visugu padi padi, inka sampadhindhinchindhi chalu india lo settle avudham anukunevallu vacchestharu gani..."

"vacchina prathivodini pattukoni trump pampesadu anukunte ela."

"India lo nenu job cheyyakunda edho okati chesukoni brathikeyocchu..."

"kani US lo ala nadavadhu... manaki kavalsindhi cheyyalem..."

"adhi trump vacchina tarvatha kadhu, enno years nunchi unna issue ae."

---

## THE BIGGER TRUTH

"US lo mana trump mama enni rules peduthunna kuda, akkade life set chesukune vallu chala mandhi unnaru."

"And mana vallu lekapothe, most of the IT nadavadhu akkada."

"Adhi, vallaki kuda thelusu." and we should be proud of it

"So vallu ae rules pettina, just manalni limit cheyyali anegani, unna andhari pampesi, vallu bajana chesukodaniki kadhu."
