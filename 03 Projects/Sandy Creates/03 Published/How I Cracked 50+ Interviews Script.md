# How I Cracked 50+ Interviews in My Career

**Format:** Reel / Short Vlog
**Tone:** Practical, helpful, personal — framework-based

---

## HOOK

"job pothe, interview ki attend avvali... idhi andhariki telusu....But aa interviews ki ela prepare avvali anedhe chala mandhi theliyani vishayam."

"Nenu naa career lo enno interviews crack chesa... because i have a framework i created."

"last varaku chudandi, oka adhiripoye bonus tip istha..."

---

## STEP 1 — Write Down the Technologies

"Oka paper thisukoni, miru resume lo pettali anukunna technologies anni oka list la dhani midha rayandi."

"aa technology miku theliyakapoyina sare."

"for example as a java developer nenu konni stack pettukunna —"

"java, spring boot,hibernate, AWS, Jenkins, Angular... ila chala"

---

## STEP 2 — Practical Knowledge Beats Technical Knowledge

"naku experience leni technologies paina mi laptop lo oka chinna project cheyyandi."

"Project ante edho ani bayapadoddhu... youtube open chesi aa technology peru kotti project ani kodithe, projects from scratch chese videos boledu untay... avi just chusthu replicate cheyyandi anthe..."

"just copy cheyyadam kadhu.. endhuku chesthunnaro oka document lo rayandi"

"complete ayina tarvatha manam rasukunna list nunchi adhi strike off cheyyandi."

"Watch how you are progressing."

---

## STEP 3 — Before Every Interview

"prathi interview mundhu ee documents anni just ala paipaina scan cheyyandi"

"interview lo most of interviewers miru technology ela use chesaro adugutharu..."

"so mi mini project ni mi project lo use chesinattu explain cheyyandi..."

"show the knowledge you have."

---

## STEP 4 — Record the Interviews

"naa interviews anni nenu record chesukune vadini."

"prathi question ni oka document lo raasi, dhaniki answer thelikapothe thelusukoni rase vadini."

"oka point tarvatha, miku same questions malli malli repeat avuthay..."

"so you will keep getting better in answering them."

"prathi interview mundhu ee document ni oka scan chesi vellandi."


Bonus tip.. miru prepare chesukunna questions anni claude or chatgpt ki icchi, mimmalni interview cheyyamanandi... keep practicing with it. Using AI in your interview preparation is life changing

---

And at last, Believe in Yourself

"Nuv select avvaledhu ante, nikanna baga cheppina vadu evado vallaki dorikaru ani..."

"so next time aa place lo nuv undadaniki effort pettu.."

"Job pothe aa stress ela untadho i know... but its not the end. so keep grinding."
