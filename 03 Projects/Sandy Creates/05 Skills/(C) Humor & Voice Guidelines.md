# Humor & Voice Guidelines for Sandy Creates

> **Core principle:** You're not a comedian. You're a relatable person being honest + self-aware. The humor comes from YOUR perspective, not from trying to be funny.

---

## When to Use Humor (Always, but strategically)

### ✅ USE HUMOR FOR:
- **Self-deprecation** — Admitting your flaws, mistakes, or contradictions. This builds trust. *Example: "I came to India with a gym plan. I have a gym plan. The gym has never met me."*
- **Relatable contradictions** — The gap between what you planned vs. what actually happened. *Example: "I'm gonna get healthy in India. [Cut to biryani.] This is healthy right?"*
- **Honest reactions** — Your genuine, unfiltered response to something. *Example: The weighing scale dread face.*
- **Callbacks** — Visual or verbal jokes that repeat and build. *Example: The unused gym shoes appearing twice.*
- **Breaking the fourth wall** — Acknowledging something awkward or funny in real time. *Example: Laughing at yourself mid-sentence.*

### ❌ DON'T USE HUMOR FOR:
- Punching down at anyone (people, groups, cultures)
- Being mean or cutting anyone down
- Forcing a joke when the moment is genuine/sincere
- Over-explaining a funny thing (kills it instantly)
- Motivational speaker tone (you're not a guru, you're a friend)

---

## The Humor Formula

Every piece of good humor in your content follows this pattern:

1. **Setup** — Establish an expectation (I came to India with a plan)
2. **Reality** — Show what actually happened (I ate biryani every day)
3. **Reaction** — Your honest, unfiltered response (Laughing at yourself, admitting defeat)

*That's it. No punchline needed. The humor is in the honesty.*

---

## Voice Rules (Always Apply)

| Aspect | What to Do | Example |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Tone** | Soft, energetic, warm, funny | Not aggressive or preachy |
| **Speed** | Talk like a friend, not a presenter | Natural pace, pauses for effect |
| **Authenticity** | Real before polished | Better to be honest than perfect |
| **Relatability** | Speak AS the audience, not AT them | "We all do this, right?" not "You should do this" |
| **Humor frequency** | Every 10-15 seconds (roughly) | Keep the energy up without overdoing it |

---

## For Future Scripts

**Always ask these questions:**

1. **Where's the contradiction?** What's the gap between expectation and reality?
2. **Where can I laugh at myself?** Where's the honest, self-aware moment?
3. **What's the callback?** Is there a visual or verbal thing that repeats and gets funnier?
4. **Am I being mean?** Is this humor punching down? (If yes, cut it.)
5. **Does it sound like me?** Would I actually say this to a friend? (If no, rewrite it.)

---

## Energy Check for Scripts

Before you film or finish editing, ask:

- [ ] Does this make me smile/laugh when I read it?
- [ ] Would someone watching feel like they're hanging out with a friend?
- [ ] Is the humor organic to the moment, or forced?
- [ ] Are there enough pauses for reactions/laughs to land?
- [ ] Does the energy match the message? (Lighter topic = more jokes; deeper topic = fewer but smarter jokes)
