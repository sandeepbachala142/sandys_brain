# What Actually Changes When You Make More Money

## Raw Idea
Everyone thinks money fixes things. It doesn't — it just changes which problems you have. US salary, still stressed. Back in India, different stress. The skill of handling life doesn't come from money.

## Hook
"US lo chala earn chesanu... and nenu anukunnantha happy avvaledu. Ela?"

Money doesnt give you happiness... it gives you freedom to do anything. 

## Angle
Relatable to anyone who's chased a number — a salary target, a savings goal, a milestone. The idea isn't that money doesn't matter (it does), but that the *mindset* you bring to money is what actually determines how you feel. Sandy can speak honestly from personal experience without being preachy. Keep it real — not a "money doesn't matter" lecture, but a "here's what I actually noticed" story.
