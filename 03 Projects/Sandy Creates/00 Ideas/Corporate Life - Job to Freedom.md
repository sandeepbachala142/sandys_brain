# Corporate Life — From Job to Freedom

## Sandy's Raw Story
starting lo job vacchinappudu edho prove chesukovali, edho podicheseyali ani thega kastapade vadini... kani problem ekkada vacchindhi ante, nuv entha prove chesukunna, inka inka prove chesukodaniki kastapaduthune undali... konni rojula tarvatha corporate job lo oka mechanical life ayipoyindhi... 

I know chala mandhi valla job ni love chestharu.. and i was one of those persons... kani oka saturation point vacchaka, job anedhi fun nunchi nannu naa family nunchi dooram ga pette burden ayipoyindhi... prashantam ga nidra poyevadini kadhu, job pothe enti ane bayam okati baga undedhi, corporate life lo chala stress undedhi... okko sari stress ekkuvaipoyi, job maneddham anukunte, naku oka family kanipisthundhi... 

oka point lo, family tho kuda time spend cheyyalenu jobs ni chesthu, enni days brathukutham ani...

Aa time lo ne i thought of looking for other source of incomes... 

Job ni vadhilesi, I am sorry Job Poyi, india ki vacchesaka for the first time in the last 15years, i am living a peaceful life... 

Miru kuda mi job ni oka fun ga kakunda oka stress ga feel avuthunte... trust me, its time to look for other source of income... miku nacchina dhantlo or any passive income... maha ayithe emavuthundhi, you will have a story in your life while chasing your dream to tell someone. 

Like i have my story now.... :)

## The Arc
1. Started with fire — prove chesukovali, podicheseyali
2. Trap — entha prove chesina, inka prove cheyyali... never ends
3. Mechanical life — job became burden, not fun
4. Fear — "job pothe enti" — golden handcuffs
5. Breaking point — family time gone, sleep gone
6. The shift — started looking for other income sources
7. Now — first peaceful life in 15 years
8. Message — if your job feels like stress not fun, start looking for alternatives. Worst case? You'll have a story. :)

## Angle
This is not an anti-job video. It's an honest story of what corporate life does over time — and what's possible on the other side. The "job pothe enti" fear is something every salaried person carries. Sandy naming it out loud is the hook.
