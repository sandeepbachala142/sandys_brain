# Why Being the Worst Person in the Room is a Gift

## Raw Idea
Sandy was the worst student in college — zero coding knowledge, 2 backlogs — and ended up outperforming people who were "better" on paper. Being behind forces you to learn faster than everyone else.

## Hook
"Nenu maa college lo worst student... and that's exactly why I grew faster than most."

## Angle
Direct follow-up energy to the Stranger/TCS story. Counterintuitive hook — people expect you to be ashamed of being the worst, not celebrate it. The point: when you're the best in the room, you coast. When you're the worst, you have no choice but to level up. Sandy has the receipts — worst student, got the job, built a life.
