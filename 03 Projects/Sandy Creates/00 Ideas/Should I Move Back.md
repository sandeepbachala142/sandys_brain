# Should I Move Back? — Responding to DMs

## Raw Notes
US nunchi india ki move ayipothanu,  Opt avvagane, loan avvagane move ayipotha… — ivi naku vacchina dms

Ofcourse cheppina kuda move avvadam anedhi antha easy decision kadhankondi.. but let me tell you something.

India ki move avvadam anedhi eppudaina oka strategic decision ayi undali… 
US lo visa tension lu, ippudu AI valla and mana trump mama valla job layoff lu… chala mandhiki vaccheyalane untundhi… but akkada aagipovadaniki karanam, "india vacchi em cheyyali"... 

and Mana dhaggara antha savings unnaya ani. ippati varaku nenu idhi cheppaledhu... but nenu india ki ravali ani 2020 lo ne decide ayyanu... and 5 years dhani paina work chesanu... 

Venakki move ayipovadam ok, but dhanni work out cheyyadaniki, manam em chesthunnam anedhi chala important. You should have a plan. 

miru mostly evarini adigina 5 yrs lo vacchestham ani antaru... i dont blame them.. because vallu adhi serious ga ne antaru...  kani ikkada problem em vasthundhi ante, aa 5 years lo velladaniki vallaki unna plan enti. 

miru kuda adhe answer cheppunte, miku unna plan enti? 

Planning is everything. Miku plan lekunda india ki istam vacchinattu move ayipothe, strategic decision kastha, emotional decision ayipothundhi.... And trust me, you will regret. 

So oka plan cheyyandi, and dhani paina work cheyyandi... even its saving like 3 crores, just plan how you can save 3 crores. And that gives you more confidence while doing anything.
## Angle
Answering the most common DM. Not preachy — honest. The "india vacchi em cheyyali" fear is real and valid. Sandy addresses it with empathy, not judgment. Strategic framing: move only when you have a plan, not just emotions.
