# Do we really need perfection in everything?

## Raw Notes
Monnati dhaka videos cheyyali ante, prathi video perfect undalemo, lekapothe janalu chudaremo ani okkokka video kosam chala time aalochinchi aagipoyevadini…

And you know prathi video naku cheyyadaniki 20mins kanna ekkuva pattedhi kadhu…

And they are not perfect..

Manalo chala mandhi oka panini perfect ga cheyyali bayapaduthu, cheyyakundane aagipotham… nenu kuda andhulo okadine

But then naku ardham ayyindhi, you cant do anything perfectly… so first try chey… do it… and then mould it…

## Angle
Personal confession — Sandy himself was stuck in perfectionism trap with his own videos. The irony: his videos took 20 mins and weren't perfect anyway. Lesson: do it, then mould it.
