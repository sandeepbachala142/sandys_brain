# Friends — What I Missed Most Coming Back

## Raw Notes
US nunchi vacchetappudu nenu main ga miss ayyindhi friends ni

US ki vellinappudu nenu okkadine… naku okkaru kuda thelidhu… alantidhi, nenu US nunchi vacchesthunte, nakosam emotional ayina vallu entho mandhi..

Nakunna friends batch lu kotthaga parichayam ayina

## Angle
Arrived alone, left with a whole community who showed up emotionally. The friends you make abroad hit different — built under pressure, away from home. Beautiful contrast between "came alone" and "left with so many people who cared."
