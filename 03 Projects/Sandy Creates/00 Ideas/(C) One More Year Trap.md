# The "One More Year" Trap

## Raw Idea
Most NRIs live in permanent delay mode — "one more year and I'll move back, one more year and I'll start that thing." Sandy lived this for 10 years. The hook writes itself.

## Hook
"Nenu US lo 10 years unna... prathi year 'oka year more' ante."

## Angle
Every NRI watching has said this exact thing to themselves. Life pakkana petthi future kosam live cheyyatam — the trap of deferred living. Sandy's story of 10 years in the US is the perfect proof point. Non-preachy because it's personal — Sandy isn't telling people what to do, just sharing what happened to him.
