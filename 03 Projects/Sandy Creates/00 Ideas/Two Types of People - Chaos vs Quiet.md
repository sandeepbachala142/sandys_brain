# Why Some People Think It's Better There — Two Types of People

## Raw Notes
These are 2 different zones…
There will be 2 types of people:
One who loves quiet, no traffic, no people around
Two who loves the chaos

## Angle
Simple but smart. No country is objectively better — it depends on what you're wired for. Non-judgmental, gets people to self-identify and comment which type they are. Strong comment bait.
