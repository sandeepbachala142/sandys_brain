# You Can't See the Picture When You're Inside the Frame

## Raw Idea
When you're in the middle of a hard chapter, it looks like failure. Only with distance do you see it was building something. Sandy can tie this to the move back to India, the trading transition, the content grind.

## Hook
"2 years ago nenu chala worst time lo unna... ippudu chusthe, adi jaruguthundha ani naaku theliyadhu."

## Angle
Not toxic positivity — Sandy isn't saying "everything happens for a reason." He's saying that clarity only comes *after* the moment, not during it. Honest and grounded. The move back to India looked like a step back to some people. Now it's the foundation for everything he's building. That contrast is the video.
