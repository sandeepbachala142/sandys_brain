# What Is My Niche? — I Am My Niche

## Raw Notes
Idhi nenu channel start chesinappudu naku vacchina oka question…

Appatilo manaki niche ante ento kuda thelidhu… aa tarvatha thelisindhi adhi manam ae topic midha channel run chesthunnam ani…

Ikkada naa channel lo nenu okati ani edhi fix avvaledhu kadha, mari naa nische enti? Okasari information isthunna, okasari naa life gurinchi chepthunna, okasari india gurinchi ila different different ga.. finally i found my niche… its ME… nene naa niche… nenu ento, naa thinking ento, nenu nerchukundhi ento cheppadame naa niche…

## Angle
Meta and relatable — every new creator asks this. The answer "I am my niche" is both simple and liberating. Will resonate with anyone thinking about starting a channel.
