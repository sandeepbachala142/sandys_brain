# Events — Missing India while in the US

## Raw Notes
Us lo unnappudu nenu baga miss ayinavi — India lo events

Naku telusu, entha mandhi enno events US vellaka miss ati untaru
Arey sontha engagement la ki kuda 

Okavela miru edhaina event ki thappaka velithe, and if you are feeling bored, just watch around you… mi chuttu 100 people unte, there are 100 stories going on around you…

## Angle
Every NRI missed weddings, engagements, birthdays back home. But the flip — when you do attend, look around. 100 people = 100 stories. Beautiful slice-of-life angle.
