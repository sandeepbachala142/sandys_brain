## Script

**HOOK (0–3 sec)**
*[Casual, slight smile]*
We bought a new bike

Bike ante Hayabusa range kadhu lendi — Honda Activa 125cc.
Inthaka mundhu bikes midha koncham undedhi gani, ippudu
Peddha bike gerlu marchukuntu drive cheyyali naku koncham chiraku… 
andhuke dhaggaralo thiragadaniki activa is best for us.

**THE REAL STORY (3–20 sec)**
Ikkada bandi thisukovadam special em kadhu… Kani adhi maa nana thisiccharu.

Engineering lo unnappudu, ayana chala sarlu adigaru... *"bike thisukora, thisukora"* ani.

Kani manaki aa time lo oka feeling undedhi..

Ayana antha kastha padi family ni poshisthu, nadhi and maa chelli dhi college fee kaduthu, akkadiki kavali ante akkadiki thisukelthu, family ni baga chusukovali ani intha kastapaduthunte — adhi respect cheyyakunda ayana nenu dabbulu karchu pedithe elaga ani…

So aayana offer icchina nenu vadukoledhu

---

**2011 (20–35 sec)**
India lo naa first job vacchina tarvatha —
2011 lo, EMI lo naa first bike konukkunna. Sontham ga.

Aa feeling different ga undhe.

---

**NOW (35–48 sec)**
and malli Ippudu — naku bandi thisivvali ani, maa nana thisukelli konicharu.

Aa time lo nannu andharu pisinari anukunnaru sare, dhanike stick ayyunna... 
kani ippudu dabbulu karchupettadaniki nenu aalochinchanu.
