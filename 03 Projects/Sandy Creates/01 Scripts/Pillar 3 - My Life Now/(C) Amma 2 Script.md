# Amma 2

**Format:** Reel
**Length:** ~60–75 seconds
**Tone:** Warm, funny, relatable — happy not dramatic

---

## HOOK
*(Light, fun — like telling a friend)*

"Amma ante... miru 5 minutes late ki call chesthe..."

"'Emi ayindhi, accident ayinda, phone chusavaa' ani oka breadth lo adugutundi." 😄

*(small laugh)*

"Mana andharikee oka amma undhi... feel matram same ga untundhi."

---

## MAA AMMA
*(Warm, personal — move from general to your mom)*

"Maa amma kuda exactly ila untundhi..."

"Nenu etthakapothe... bhayam tho naa friends andhariki call chesesthadhi." 😄

*(chinna pause)*

"Antha bagane untundhi gani... chaaaala mondi."

"Mata mukku sootiga untundhi gani... heart matram chala sweet."

*(smile)*

"Chinnappati nunchi... nenu edho okati avuthanu ani..."

"Maa amma matram baga nammedhi."

"Naa matalu... naa singing... anni maa amma dhaggara nunche vacchay."

---

## ENDING
*(Turn it back — warm, curious)*

"Miru mee amma gurinchi aalochinchagane..."

"Miku gurthocchindhi enti??" ❤️

"Comment box lo cheppandi."

"See you in the next one." 🙂
