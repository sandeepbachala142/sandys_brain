# How Is Lucky After Moving Back?

**Format:** Reel
**Length:** ~75–90 seconds
**Tone:** Warm, honest, light humour — proud dad, not an expert on parenting

> **Fill the gaps first:** anything in **[brackets]** is a detail only you know. Swap in the real one. Real beats polished. If you don't remember a detail, cut the line, don't invent it.

---

## STORY FRAMEWORK

| Step | |
|------|-|
| **Situation** | Lucky (4) grew up in the US. Daycare, car rides, one small family unit |
| **Desire** | Every parent's worry: "Will he be okay if we move?" |
| **Conflict** | New city, new school, new routine. I had no idea how he'd take it |
| **Change** | He got grandparents, a lot more family, and a school where he fit in |
| **Result** | He's doing better than I expected. Honestly, I'm the one still adjusting 😄 |
