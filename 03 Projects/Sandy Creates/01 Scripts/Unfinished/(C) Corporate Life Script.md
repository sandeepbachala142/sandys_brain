# Corporate Life — From Prove Chesukovalsi to Peaceful Life

**Format:** Reel / Short vlog
**Length:** ~90 seconds
**Tone:** Honest, personal, warm — not anti-job, just real

---

## HOOK
*(Camera on face, calm)*
"Oka peddha IT company lo job vacchinappudu..."

*(chinna pause)*

"Edho prove chesukovali... edho podicheseyali ani thega kastapade vadini."

*(pause)*

"Kani problem enti ante..."

"Nuv entha prove chesukunna... inka inka prove chesukodaniki kastapaduthune undali."

---

## THE TRAP
*(Honest, no drama)*

"Konni rojula tarvatha..."

"Corporate job lo oka mechanical life ayipoyindhi."

*(chinna pause)*

"I know chala mandhi valla job ni love chesthuntaru..."

"Nenu kuda andhulo okadi la undevadini"

*(pause)*

"Kani oka saturation point vacchaka..."

"Job anedhi fun nunchi... nannu naa family nunchi... dooram ga pette burden ayipoyindhi."

---

## THE FEAR
*(Quiet, real)*

"Prashantam ga nidra poyevadini kaadu."

*(chinna pause)*

"Job pothe enti... ane oka bayam baga undedhi."

*(pause)*

"Oka point lo... family tho kuda time spend cheyyalekapoyevadini."

"Inka enni days ila anipinchindhi."

---

## THE SHIFT
*(Slight relief in voice)*

"Aa time lo ne..."

"Other sources of income choodadam start chesanu."

*(pause)*

"Job ni vadhilesi... Sorry Sorry, Na job poyi..."

"US nunchi India ki vacchesaka..."

*(chinna pause)*

"First time in 15 years... life ni stress lekunda oka peaceful angle nunchi chusthunnanu"

"Na job gurinchi... its a different story. Adhi inko video lo complete ga chepthanu."

---

## THE MESSAGE
*(Warm, direct — not preachy)*

"Miru mi job ni fun way lo kakunda, stress ga feel avuthunte..."

"Trust me... its time to look for other sources of income."

*(chinna pause)*

"Miku nacchina dhantlo... any passive income..."

"Job manesi try cheyyamani nenu cheppadam ledhu..."
"But job unnappude inkokati set chesukovali ani chepthunnanu."

*(soft smile)*

"Maha ayithe emavuthundhi?"

*(pause)*

"You will have a story to tell while chasing your dream."

*(chinna pause)*

"Like I have mine now." 🙂

"See you in the next one."
