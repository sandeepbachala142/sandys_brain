# Do We Really Need Perfection in Everything?

**Format:** Reel / Short vlog
**Length:** ~60–90 seconds
**Tone:** Honest, self-deprecating, warm

---

## HOOK
*(Camera on face, direct eye contact)*

"Oka confession cheyyalani undhi…"

*(chinna pause)*

"Nenu chala sarlu ee camera on chesi… off chesanu."

---

## SETUP
*(Casual, talking like a friend)*

"Prathi video chese mundhu okate feeling."

"Ee video perfect ga undali… lighting sari ga undali… cheppedhi perfect ga undali…
lekapothe janalu chuddaru ane oka feeling."

*(soft laugh)*

"Anni correct ga chesukoni, anni perfect ga chusukoni, appudu cheddhamu ani
wait chesthunna…"

*(pause)*

"Last ki cheyyadam manesthunna."

---

## THE TRUTH
*(Slight smile, honest)*

"Miku oka vishayam cheppali…"

"Naa prathi video cheyyadaniki… 20 minutes kanna ekkuva pattedhi kaadu."

*(pause — let that land)*

"And they are not perfect."

*(chinna pause)*

"Kani… chala mandhi chustunnaru."

---

## THE REAL STORY
*(Warm, relatable)*

"Manalo chala mandi ila untam…"

"Oka pani cheyyali ante… perfect ga cheyyali ani bayapaduthu…
cheyyakunda aagipotham."

"Nenu kuda andhulo okadine."

*(pause)*

"Naku ardham ayyindhi enti ante…"

"Mana first step perfect ga undali ante… oka adugu kuda veyyalem."

---

## THE LESSON
*(Not preachy — just honest)*

"Ippati nunchi follow avvalanukuntundhi okate…"

*(chinna pause)*

"First… DO it."

"Don't wait for perfection."

*(pause)*

"It will never be perfect."

*(chinna pause)*

"Perfection anedhi starting lo ne radhu…"

"Slow ga… slow ga… Mana experience and learnings tho develop avuthundhi."

*(soft smile)*

"Ee video kuda perfect kaadu… kani meeru chustunnaru kadha." 😄

---

## CTA
*(Direct, warm)*

"Miru kuda ila feel ayyara??"

"Oka pani start cheyyali ani… anni perfect ayyaka start cheddham ani aagipoyara??"

"Comment box lo cheppandi… atleast nenu okkadine kaadhu koncham happy feel avutha." 😄

"See you in the next one."

---

## B-ROLL NOTES
- Camera on/off moment at the start — show yourself hesitating
- Casual setting — room, no fancy setup needed
- End with a relaxed smile, not a dramatic one
