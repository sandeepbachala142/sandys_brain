# Lucky's Birthday — 4 Years & First Time with Family

**Format:** Reel / Short Vlog
**Length:** ~90 seconds
**Tone:** Warm, emotional, celebratory — proud dad, grateful son

---

## HOOK
*(Direct to camera, warm smile)*

"Recent ga maa lucky gadi 4th birthday ayyindhi."

*(pause)*

"First time vadi birthday ikkada family tho celebrate cheyyadam."

"Andhuke maa vadi birthday anagane, maa amma, nanna, maa atthayya vallu, maa ammamma andharu parigetthukoni hyderabad vacchesaru"

"Lucky gadi birthday roje inko special enti ante..."

"Ade roje maa ammamma birthday kuda."

"First time... valla iddharidhi kalisi celebrate cheyyadam."
i know boledu first times unnay....
US lo unnappudu chala sarlu anukunna, maa  lucky gadi birthday 
ikkada maa vallatho eppudu celebrate chesthano ani.... 

Every year try cheyyadam, edho okati issue vacchi backout avvadam. 

But ee sari chala chala baga anipinchindhi... 

Nijam ga cheppali ante, lucky gadiki kuda intha family undhi anipinchindhi..

---

"Aa roju manchiga Birla Mandir ki velli."

"Necklace road antha oka round yesi, 

rendu cakes thisukoni,  mana sandeep kishan resturant Vivaha bojanambu ki velipoyam"

"Full ga thini cake cutting chesam"

"Oka roju... rendu generations... rendu birthdays."

"Naaku aa moment chala special ga anipinchindhi."

"Dhini kosame nenu US nunchi vacchesindhi ani cheppanu gani, rakapoyi unte, "Next year ayina veedi birthday india lo cheyyali" ani 4th time anukunevallam.... anthe... 

"Celebrating some occasions with family is worth it. 
miru prapancham lo ekkada unna, appudappudu ayina try cheyyandi... 
Its completely worth it"

"See you all in the next one." 🙂
