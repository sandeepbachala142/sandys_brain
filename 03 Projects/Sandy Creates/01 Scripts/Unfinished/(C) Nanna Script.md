# Meet My Nanna

**Format:** Reel
**Length:** ~60-75 seconds
**Tone:** Warm, light humour, emotional ending

---

## HOOK
*(Camera on face, easy smile)*

"Meet my nanna..."

*(chinna pause)*

"Mikemaina kastalu unnaya??"

"Maa nanna dhaggaraki velli kurchondi..."
"Edho oka joke ki navvuthuntaru." 😄

---

## THE EMCET STORY
*(Laughing slightly as you tell it)*

"Emcet lo chadivi rank kotti... college lo seat thecchukuntanani cheppu ra..."
"Ani maa amma adigithe..."

*(pause - smile building)*

"Ekkuva tension padaku nanna... ni chaduvu gurinchi naku telusu..."
"Ani management quota lo seat konna type maa nanna." 😄

---

## THE REAL NANNA
*(Tone shifts -- warm, emotional)*

"Kani oka vishayam cheptha..."

"Nenu life lo ae risk chesthanu anna... oka dhairyam ga naa venke untaru."

*(pause)*
Anthendhuku
"US nunchi india ki vasthunna ani chepthe kuda..."
"Em parledhu... Happy ga occhesey, em lekapoyina, nenu oka business set chestha... annaru."

*(pause - emotional)*

"That's my nanna."

Lucky gaditho nenu time spend chesthunnappudu, and vadi life ela untundho ani aalochisthunnappudu, anipisthu untundhi.. maa nanna kuda naa chinnappudu naa gurinchi ilane aalochinchuntaru kadha ani... 



---

## ENDING
*(Quiet, genuine)*

"Meeru mee nanna gurinchi aalochinchagane... miku first gurthocchindhi enti??"

"Comment box lo cheppandi." ❤️

"See you in the next one." 🙂
