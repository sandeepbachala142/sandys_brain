# Food Food Food

**Format:** Reel
**Length:** ~60-75 seconds
**Tone:** Fun, self-deprecating, high energy

---

## HOOK
*(Big smile, direct to camera)*

"India lo Diet. Gym. Healthy routine."

"India ki vasthunna ani... oka plan chesukunnanu."

*(pause - then laugh)*

"Aa plan ki emi ayyindo ippudu cheptha." 😄

---

## THE REALITY
*(Animated, enjoying it)*

"Hyderabad lo — Niloufer chai, bun maska..., Shah Ghouse Biriyani..."

*(chinna pause)*

"Nellore ki vellithe — Neyyi karam dosa, badam kheer, honey cake..."

*(pause)*

"Intiki vasthe — mutton, chicken..."

*(pause)*

"Daavuda." 😄

---

## THE CONFESSION
*(Laughing at yourself)*

"Assalu gap ledhu."

*(chinna pause)*

"Prathi dhaggara... prathi illu... prathi food..."

"Edhi padithe adhi thinnanu."

*(pause)*

"Weighing scale meeda niluchodaniki bayamesi, asalu weight chudadam manesa" 😄

---

## THE PLAN (AGAIN)
*(Slightly dramatic)*

"Ippudu current oka routine inka ledhu..."

*(chinna pause)*

"But oka routine start ayyaka... diet ayithe kacchitam ga start cheyyali."

"Because health is everything."

"India ki vasthe food control cheyyadam... koncham kashtame guys."

---

## CTA
*(Warm, fun)*

"Miru india ki vasthe 1 month ki casual ga entha weight gain avutharu??"

*(pause)*

"Comment box lo cheppandi... cheppandi... cheppandi." 😄

"See you in the next one." 🙂

---

## B-ROLL NOTES
- Show the actual food — Niloufer, Shah Ghouse if you have clips/photos
- Weigh scale moment — dramatic look at it
- Gym shoes sitting unused in the corner — comedy visual
- Keep cuts fast, energy high throughout
