# Should I Move Back? — Responding to DMs

**Format:** Reel
**Length:** ~90 seconds
**Tone:** Honest, empathetic, direct — not preachy. Real talk from someone who actually did it.

---

## HOOK
*(Direct to camera — reading DMs tone)*

"US nunchi india ki move ayipothanu..."

"Opt avvagane move ayipotha..."

"Loan avvagane move ayipotha..."

*(pause)*

"Ivi naku vacchina DMs."

*(chinna pause)*

"Ofcourse... cheppina kuda move avvadam antha easy decision kaadhu."

"But let me tell you something."

---

## THE REAL REASON PEOPLE STAY
*(Calm, no judgment)*

"US lo visa tensions... AI valla layoffs... Trump mama valla Chala mandhiki vaccheyalane untundhi..."

*(pause)*

"But akkada aagipovadaniki karanam, okke oka question..."

*(pause for effect)*

"India vacchi... em cheyyali?"

---

## SANDY'S STORY
*(Personal, honest)*

"Ee question naku kuda vacchindhi."

*(pause)*

"Kani nenu india ki ravali ani... 2020 lo ne decide ayyanu."

"And aa decision paina... 5 years work chesanu."

*(chinna pause)*

"Savings, planning and income sources... anni chusukunnake india moving plan implement chesanu."

---

## THE REAL PROBLEM
*(Direct, not judging)*

"Miru evarini adigina... '5 years lo vacchestham' antaru."
and common ga aa question miru icche reaction " aa chala mandhini chusam le" ani

*(pause)*

"ala cheppe vallani nenu ayithe blame cheyyanu... endhukante aa time lo adhi genuien  ga cheppe matale"

*(pause — the key question)*

"Kani oka vishayam adugutanu..."

"Miru kuda adhe answer cheppunte..."

"aa 5 yrs lo velladaniki Miku unna plan enti?"

---

## THE MESSAGE
*(Warm but firm)*

"Plan lekunda... istam vacchinattu move ayipothe..."

"Strategic decision kaadhu... emotional decision avuthundhi."

*(pause)*

"And trust me... you will regret."

*(chinna pause)*

"So oka plan cheyyandi... dhani paina work cheyyandi."

"Even if it's saving 3 crores..."

"Just plan cheyyi... ela save cheyyalo."

*(pause — soft)*

"Aa plan ne... miku confidence isthundhi."

"Everything ki."
