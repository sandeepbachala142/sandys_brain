## HOW MUCH TO START WITH
*(Personal, grounded)*

"Firstly miru month ki entha invest cheyyalo oka amount fix anukondi."

"Nenu 340$ per month tho start chesa." 

---

## STEP 1 — BROKERAGE ACCOUNT
*(Simple, clear)*

"Stocks konadaniki first kavalsindhi oka brokerage account."

"US lo oka beginner ayina use cheyyagaligela unde brokerage account — Robinhood."

"So App Store ki velli, simple ga Robinhood account create chesukondi."

---

## STEP 2 — DIVIDE YOUR AMOUNT
*(Practical)*

"Manam fix ayina amount ni oka 4 parts ga divide chesukondi."

"And aa 4 parts ni 4 stocks lo recurring deposit pettandi."

"Recurring ante every month mi account nunchi aa money automatic ga deduct  ayyi, ee stocks lo invest ayipothundhi." So miku comfortable unde amount matrame pettandi... 
Dhinne SIP antaru... 

---

## STEP 3 — SET UP RECURRING INVESTMENT
*(Step by step — screen recording here)*

"Robinhood lo recurring pettadam chala simple."

Nenu mostly 4 stocks lo deposit chesevadini.. "SPY, AAPL, TSLA and VOO"
"account open chesaka, Kindha search click chesi, SPY ane stock ni search chesi first dhi select cheyyandi — its an index fund."

"Kindha trade button click chesi, paina dollars press chesi, recurring investment select chesi, Every month ni select cheyyandi."

"Prathi month ee stock lo entha invest cheyyali anukuntunnaro type chesi, submit cheyyandi."

"Alage AAPL, TSLA, VOO lo kuda cheseyocchu."

"Anthe... congratulations!! You have done your first investment."


## THE WARNING
*(Honest, important)*

"Kani guys... starting lo huge amount invest cheddham ane aalochana pettukokandi."

"First slow ga start cheyyandi.."

"Oka 5 months asalu em chesthunnaro miku ardham ayina tarvatha — you can increase the amount slowly."

"And idhi long term investment ki... roju open chesi, perigindha, thaggindha ani chusi tension padakandi."

"idhi next 5 to 10 years tarvatha mana future kosam manam chesthunna oka commitment."
