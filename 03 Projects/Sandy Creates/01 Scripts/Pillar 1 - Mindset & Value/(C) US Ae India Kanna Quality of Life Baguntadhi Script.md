**Format:** Reel

> **Fill the gaps first:** anything in **[brackets]** is a detail only you know. Swap in the real one or cut the line.

## STORY FRAMEWORK

| Step | |
|------|-|
| **Situation** | 10 years in the US, then moved back to Hyderabad. Everyone asks: "Which is better?" |
| **Desire** | A straight answer, and I wanted one too |
