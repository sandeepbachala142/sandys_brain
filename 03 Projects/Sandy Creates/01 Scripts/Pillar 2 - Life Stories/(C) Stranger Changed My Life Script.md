# The Stranger Who Changed My Life

**Format:** Reel / Short Vlog
**Length:** ~90–120 seconds
**Tone:** Warm, storytelling, genuine — ends on a karma/reflection note

---

## HOOK

"life lo oka goal pettukoni, Idhi manam cheyyalem anukune valla kosam ee video "

"Kushi cinema lo pawan kalyan story oka stranger valla change ayinattu, naa life oka stranger valla maripoyina story "

## THE ONE SHOT — TCS
*(Building tension)*

"maa college ki vacchina first company TCS."

"Ikkada important thing entante, TCS thappa inka ae company backlogs unde vallani allow cheyyaru.. so it's my only shot. select avvakapothe inka life biscuit ae."

"ECE background ayina naku computer coding midha zero knowledge."

---

## THE EXAM ROOM
*(Turning point)*

"interview lo first round aptitude..."

"exam start avvaka mundhu, naa pakkana okadu chala tension padipothunnadu..."

"enti bro ani adigithe — ee okka round pass ayithe chalu, technical round lo nenu crack chesestha bro' annadu..."

"I know it's wrong but I helped him... I solved his complete paper."

"iddharam first round pass ayipoyam."

"bayataki vacchaka he was very very happy "

---

## THE TWIST
*(Emotional, grateful)*

"but next round technical...  dhantlo elagaina nenu pothanani naku thelusu.."

"'ponile atleast nuv ayina job kottu bro ani thanatho annappudu'"

"he took me into a classroom and nannu kurchobetti — without even a break more than 8 hours lo — Java basics nunchi 100 programs varaku logical ga explain chesthu nerpinchadu."

*(genuine)*

"next day technical round ki interviewer icchina 3 programs ni complete chesi,  logical ga explain chesa."

"he was impressed. and I got a job."

## THE ENDING
*(Reflective, warm)*

"aa roju tarvatha I never saw him again."

"Naku athani peru thelidhu ooru thelidhu, but he made me do what I thought was impossible for me"

ae goal manam acheive cheyyalenantha bigger kaadu..
