# The Day We Decided to Move Back

**Format:** Reel / Short Vlog
**Length:** ~90 seconds
**Tone:** Personal, emotional, honest — the real story

---

**HOOK (0–5 sec)**
The day when we decided to move back to India... 

---

**SETTING THE SCENE (5–20 sec)**
Nov 2025...  naa entire US journey lo ne life peaceful ga pothunna time adhi... 

Piccha lite ga unde job... maa lucky gadu roju daycare ki velthunde vadu... naa wife appudappude manchi ga car nerchukoni drive chesthundedhi.. 

Financially kuda oka happy time lo unna.

India move ayye thoughts ayithe chala strong ga undevigani, aleast inko 2yrs unta anukunna... 

---

**THE CALL (20–40 sec)**
morning antha meetings finish chesukoni, prashanthanga kurchoni stock market chusthu unna...

and then maa vendor dhaggara nunchi call osthunnindhi...

US lo niku ni vendor call chesthunnadu ante, 95% edho penta peduthunnadu ani ardham.... 
call lift chesaka, repe ni last day annadu.... 

nenem matladaledhu... ok thank you ani cheppesa.. endhukante usually vendors job poyindhi ani cheppina tarvatha inka discussions anavasaram.

---

**THE CONFESSION (40–55 sec)**
nenu miku okati frank ga cheppali... actually nenu aa job povadam kosame wait chesthuuu unna... adhi pothe india ki velipodham ani... Because i was fully prepared...

kani adhi mari antha sudden ga vacchesariki, oka 10 mins silent ga kurchoni undipoya....

naa mind lo boledu thoughts... india ki nijam ga ne velipodhama.. US lo undhama...
but US lo undali ante inko job vethakali.. naaku aa interest ledhu...

---

**THE CONVERSATION (55–75 sec)**
kindhaki vacchi "Niko news... job poyindhi" ani cheppa... thanu 2 mins shock lo silent ayipoyindhi...
next enti ani adigindhi... "India velipodham" ani cheppa... 
malli oka 5mins silence.... and then, ok velipodham anindhi... 

---

**THE REALITY (75–90 sec)**
anthe....Decision ayithe fast ga thisukunnam kani, aa decision ni digest chesukodaniki maaku 1 month pattindhi... 

okate thoughts
Nijam ga velthunnama... 
Vellaka ela untadho... Maa lucky gadiki elago... thondara paduthunnama....  inka chala chala thoughts....

memedho easy ga move ayipoyam ani chala mandhi anukuntaru gani, its was very tough...

But now i dont regret my decision.... 
Nenu india ki move avvamani evvariki cheppanu... endhukante mana life mottham malli restart cheyyadam antha easy kadhu... 
but evarikaina nijam ga move avvali anukunte, my videos might help you guys to understand the reality. 

Inthaki miru ekkada nunchi naa videos chusthunnaru?? comment box lo cheppandi... 
alage follow kottandi...

See you all in the next one.
