# My Rental House Update in USA

**Format:** Reel
**Length:** ~90–100 seconds
**Tone:** Honest, light humour, practical. A "here's what actually happened" update, not advice

> **Before you record, check:**
> 1. **[Brackets]** are details only you know. Swap in the real one or cut the line.
> 2. **Numbers on screen.** I used rent ($2,500), mortgage (~$2,000) and management fee (8%) from your Financials notes. Decide if you're comfortable sharing them. The "How much money I saved" reel shows people want exact numbers, but it's your call.
> 3. **Leasing fee.** Your notes say a one-month leasing fee ($2,500) was owed to the new manager, and you still need to confirm whether it was deducted from the first rent. Don't say the net math on camera until that's confirmed.
> 4. **No names.** I left out tenants' names, the property managers' company names, the address and the trust. Keep it that way.
