The account grew on one story: a Telugu family moving back from the USA to India. Since the move ended, the content has spread into habits, motivation, politics and interview tips, and views have fallen 5–10x.

---

## 1. What's Working

- **High-stakes, specific moments from the move.** Losing the job, selling the car, closing bank/SSN/401k and shipping the house each did 85K–661K. People want to watch someone actually make the decision they're considering.
- **Telugu hooks.** "USA Raavala… Vaddha…?", "Antha OK na?" and "Asalu will…" make it clear who this is for. That's a strength.
- **US stocks from India/abroad.** "How to invest in US stocks" got 151K and "How to invest/trade US stocks outside country" got 108K. Trading works when it's tied to the NRI angle.
- **Milestone check-ins after the move.** "100 days in Hyderabad" (67.2K) and "How much money I saved to move back" (51.9K) are the best recent posts. They continue the story.
- **A recognisable look.** The bold white-box title text makes the grid easy to spot.

## 2. What's Not Working

- **Generic self-help.** "Habit Tracker" (10.6K), "Can you do it?" (10.2K) and "I celebrated my efforts" (14.4K) could come from any creator, so they get no credit from the NRI story.
- **Off-niche commentary.** The Trump reel got 22.3K and doesn't connect to why people followed.
- **The generic investing series.** "Investing 1/2/3" got 21K–38.9K. It's textbook content, and it got a fraction of what the specific US-stocks-from-India reels got.
- **Letterboxed footage.** Many recent reels are horizontal clips inside a black 9:16 frame, so about 40% of the screen is empty. The older face-to-camera reels fill the frame and did much better.

## 3. What to Change

- **Bio:** Currently reads "Personal blog / Exploring Life / Music lover / Dream chaser / Professional bathroom singer / Stocks Freak / Married to the boss / Dad of a mini troublemaker." Nothing there tells a new visitor why to follow. Suggested rewrite:
  > 🇺🇸 10 yrs USA → 🇮🇳 Hyderabad with family
  > Real numbers on moving back: money, jobs, kids, US assets
  > 📈 Trading US markets from India
  > Telugu 🎙️
- **Name field:** "Sandeep Bachala" isn't searchable. Use something like "Sandeep | USA → India 🇮🇳" so the profile shows up for "NRI" or "moving back to India" searches.
- **Category:** Change "Personal blog" to Creator or Digital creator.
- **Link:** It's a raw YouTube URL with the channel ID, and the channel isn't active yet. Until it is, link to something that captures interest, like a "moving back checklist" or a link hub. Switch to YouTube once videos are up.
- **Pinned reels:** Currently "What is this channel??" (35.9K) and "Corporate Life". Replace with "Thank you USA" (661K), "How I lost my job" (412K), plus the best post-move reel.
- **Highlights:** "Moving to 🇮🇳" is 8th, behind "Bahamas", "Canada" and "Friends". Move it to first. Add "Money", "US Stocks" and "Life in Hyd". Archive or merge the vacation ones.

## 4. What to Make More Of

1. **"Back in India" milestones:** 6 months back, 1 year back, "Would I move back again?" The 100-day reel shows this format works.
2. **Honest money comparisons:** US salary vs Hyderabad salary, rent, school fees, groceries. "How much money I saved" already shows the appetite.
3. **Running US assets from India:** 401k, US bank accounts, taxes, credit history. Picks up where the 148K "Bank accounts/SSN/401k" reel left off.
4. **Trading US markets from India, from your own experience:** trading hours, setup, real P&L lessons. Keep it personal rather than "Investing – Part 1".
5. **Lucky adjusting to India:** "Can Lucky adjust?" got 68.9K. Kid's school, language and friends updates hit every parent thinking about moving back.
6. **Telugu "should you come back?" debates:** "USA Raavala Vaddha" style, answering real DMs from NRIs still in the US.

## 5. The Biggest Things Holding the Account Back

1. **The story ended and nothing replaced it.** The move was the series. Now there's no "why follow me next." The next chapter is "life after moving back, with real numbers," and every reel should fit it.
2. **The profile doesn't convert viral viewers.** Someone who sees the 661K reel lands on a bio that says "Professional bathroom singer" and a pinned "What is this channel??". That's where followers are lost.
3. **Too many topics at once.** Motivation, habits, politics, interviews, investing basics, family vlogs. The algorithm and new viewers can't tell what the account is about. Cut to three: NRI return life, money, and US trading from India.
