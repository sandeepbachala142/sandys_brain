# Sandy's Voice Guide
> Claude reads this before writing any script. This is the law.

---

## Language

**Telugu-English mix** — scripts are primarily in Telugu (Roman script), with English words dropped in naturally for emphasis, technical terms, or punchy moments. Never fully English. Never fully Telugu. The mix *is* the voice.

Examples of natural switching:
- "Nenu financial advisor ni kaanu… nenu just naa journey share chesthunna."
- "After a long time I felt like I am the owner of the TV. 😄"
- "Clarity before courage."

---

## Tone

| What Sandy IS | What Sandy is NOT |
|---|---|
| Soft and warm | Loud or dramatic |
| Energetic but calm | Preachy or lecture-y |
| Honest and personal | Polished or formal |
| Funny in a self-deprecating way | Sarcastic or mean |
| Relatable — "guy sharing his life" | Expert giving advice |

---

## Humor Style

Self-deprecating, casual, never forced. The joke comes from his own situation — never at anyone else's expense.

**Real examples from his scripts:**
- *"Intlo nenu okkadine… After a long time I felt like I am the owner of the TV. 😄"*
- *"Naku coffee asalu alavatu ledhu, kani naa wife baga coffee chesthadhi.. Ala alavatu ayyindhi"*
- *"ee auction lo naa car $24500 ki sell ayyindhi"* — said after explaining all the stress of selling
- *"Lucky ga Qatar vallu full refund iccharu"* — relief as punchline
- *"Referral code use chesthe inko $100 gift card kuda istharanta— nenu try cheyyaledhu 😄"*

Humor placement: Usually after a tense or information-heavy moment, as a release valve.

## Script Structure

Almost every script follows this pattern:

```
1. HOOK — A question or line that stops the scroll
2. SETUP — Context, what happened, why this matters
3. STORY / EXPERIENCE — The actual journey, honest and personal
4. LESSON — Embedded naturally, never announced
5. CTA / ENDING — Comment prompt or "See you in the next one 🙂"
```

---

## Signature Phrases (use naturally, not every script)

- **"Nijam cheppali ante…"** — when being honest about something hard
- **"(pause)"** or **"(chinna pause)"** — used for rhythm and emotional beats
- **"This is my journey."** — end-of-video anchor, grounds everything as personal
- **"See you in the next one 🙂"** — almost universal sign-off
- **"Will keep you guys posted…"** — life-update endings
- **"nenu just naa journey share chesthunna"** — disclaimer without being defensive
- **"Andhariki ela unnaru?"** — casual opener for life update videos
- **"Hope ee information help ayyindhi anukuntunnanu"** — sign-off for info videos

---

## What Sandy ALWAYS Does

- **Frames everything as personal experience** — "nenu chesanu", "naa experience", "this is my story"
- **Explicit non-advice disclaimers** — "nenu financial advisor ni kaanu", "nenu just naa opinion chepthunna", "evariki suggestion kaadu"
- **Includes B-roll directions** — "(Broll here)", "(chinna pause – softer tone)", "(screen recording)" — writes cinematically
- **Ends with a comment CTA** — invites audience to respond, ask questions, share their answer
- **Uses emojis sparingly** — 😄 🙂 👋 ❤️ — never overloaded
- **Pauses for emotional peaks** — doesn't over-explain, lets silence do the work

---

## What Sandy NEVER Does

- No motivational speaker energy — never "YOU CAN DO IT" type lines
- No pointing fingers at groups, countries, or people
- No "this is the right way" — always "this is my way"
- No abusive or negative language toward anyone
- No overdramatic emotional manipulation — emotion comes from honesty, not theatrics

## Voice Check (run this before finalizing any script)

- [ ] Is it in Telugu-English mix, not full English?
- [ ] Does it sound like Sandy talking to a friend, not presenting?
- [ ] Is there at least one moment of light humor or self-deprecation?
- [ ] Is the lesson embedded in the story — not announced?
- [ ] Does it end with "See you in the next one" or a natural CTA?
- [ ] Are there pause directions for emotional beats?
- [ ] Is it framed as personal experience, not advice?
