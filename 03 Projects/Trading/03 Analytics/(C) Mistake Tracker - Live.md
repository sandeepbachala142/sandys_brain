## The Recurring Mistake Patterns

### 🔴 MISTAKE #1 — Wrong Direction on Absorption Signal
**Frequency:** 1 confirmed (May 13 - ES trade)
**Cost:** SL hit

**What happens:**
Sandy correctly spots the absorption signal (big bubbles + delta) but enters in the WRONG direction because the location context isn't assessed first.

**The rule being broken:**
> Green bubbles at LOWS = LBF = LONG
> Green bubbles at HIGHS = LAF = SHORT
> Red bubbles at LOWS = LBF = LONG
> Red bubbles at HIGHS = LAF = SHORT

**The fix:**
Before reading the bubble, ask: *"Am I at a HIGH or a LOW in the structure?"*

### 🟡 MISTAKE #2 — Counter-Trend Trade on a Trend Day
**Frequency:** 1 confirmed (May 13 - NQ trade)
**Cost:** Missed full move, small win instead of big win (or avoided big loss)

**What happens:**
Sandy correctly identifies a LBF absorption signal and enters long — but it's a strongly trending day. The bounce is small and temporary. Sandy exits correctly but the issue is taking counter-trend trades when the day type is trend.

**The rule being broken:**
> On trend days → trade WITH the trend (LAF shorts on bounces)
> On balance days → fade the extremes (LBF longs, LAF shorts at edges)

**The fix:**
Before any trade, classify the day:
- Elongated thin profile, lower highs/lows → TREND DAY → only trade with trend
- Fat balanced profile, price rotating between levels → BALANCE DAY → fade extremes

## The Pre-Trade Questions (Non-Negotiable)

Answer these in order before every entry:

**1. What day type is this?**
- Thin elongated profile → Trend day → only trade WITH trend
- Fat balanced profile → Balance day → fade edges

**2. Where am I in the structure?**
- At a HIGH after a run up → look for LAF short
- At a LOW after a flush → look for LBF long
- Mid-range → NO TRADE

**3. What does the signal say?**
- Big bubbles + delta = one direction, price doesn't follow = absorption
- Only now read the signal — AFTER answering 1 and 2

**4. Score the setup (3+/4 required)**
- [ ] Known structural level nearby
- [ ] Big bubble at the level
- [ ] Delta profile 2-3x average magnitude
- [ ] Price failed to extend
- [ ] Day type matches trade direction

**5. Is this a trend or counter-trend trade?**
- Counter-trend on trend day → max 1 try, tight target, quick exit
- With-trend on trend day → hold to next structure
- Balance day fade → hold to POC/opposite edge
