---

## The Three-Word Mantra

*"its not that complicated chat, plan wait execute"*

Everything else is detail around this.

## Pre-Market Process — How He Builds the Plan

Every morning before the session:

1. **Identify the overnight range** — OVH (Overnight High), OVL (Overnight Low)
2. **Mark key volume profile levels** — VAH, VAL, POC from previous RTH session
3. **Find LVN pockets** — where volume is thin → price will move fast there
4. **Note double distribution profiles** — two value areas from prior day = LVN between them is the target zone
5. **Define 1-2 specific price levels** to watch at open — not a dozen, 1-2 max
6. **Set the scenario** — "if we test X, I'm watching for Y"
7. **Set alerts** — so he doesn't have to stare at the screen

*"Stay patient — let price come to us."*

**What he posts in premarket:**
- Specific price zones (e.g. "watching 25300-25320")
- The WHY behind each level (LVN, VAH, prior cluster, etc.)
- His scenario: what he needs to see to trade it

## Trade Execution Rules

### Entry
- Only enter at **pre-marked levels** — never chase
- Needs orderflow confirmation at the level (absorption, tape bombs, passive buyer/seller)
- For LBF: waits for aggressive reclaim candle back through the level
- For LAF: waits for price to fail at the level and start rejecting
- Scales in: small first entry, adds on confirmation

### Stop Loss
- Placed at the level invalidation point (above the sweep high for shorts, below for longs)
- Moves to breakeven once trade is going
- *"moving my sl to +10 pts"* — moves to partial profit lock early

### Targets
- **Target 1:** POC or midpoint of range → scales out 50-80%
- **Target 2:** Next significant level (OVH/OVL, PDH/PDL, VAH/VAL of another session)
- Lets the last piece run with breakeven stop

### When to Cut Early
- *"if it doesnt [accelerate] Im cutting this B"* — no acceleration after break = cut
- "if we make a higher low of the overnight low not interested" — structure doesn't confirm
- Takes profits quicker in choppy conditions vs trending

## Hard Rules — Non-Negotiable

1. **Plan. Wait. Execute.** — always in that order, never reversed
2. **Don't trade if ES and NQ are moving opposite** at open — conflicting signals = no trade
3. **No trading before 8 PM EST overnight** on any product
4. **Sit out before news** — no entries within 2 mins of high-impact data
5. **Wait until 10 AM** when open is choppy or range-bound
6. **Don't short strong momentum** — wait for stalling/absorption signals first
7. **Don't anticipate breakouts** — *"never anticipate a breakout when market is telling you it wants to range"*
8. **Cut when no acceleration follows a break** — if price breaks a level and doesn't follow through, the thesis is wrong
9. **Be patient** — *"Patience is KEY!!!"* — repeated throughout every month

## The "Boring" Edge

Yush's edge isn't a complex system. It's the same 2-3 setups, repeated every day, at pre-marked levels, confirmed by orderflow. The edge is:

1. **Patience** — letting price come to the level
2. **Preparation** — the level is marked before price gets there
3. **Confirmation** — orderflow tells him which side is trapped
4. **Discipline** — cutting early when wrong, holding when right

*"You are not betting on a rally — you are positioning in front of one the order flow is structurally compelling the market to produce."*
