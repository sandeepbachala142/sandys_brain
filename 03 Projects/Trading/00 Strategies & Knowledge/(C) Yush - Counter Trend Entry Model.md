# (C) Yush — Counter Trend Entry Model
*Distribution → Structure Break → LH Retest → Entry*

---

## Yush's Rule

> "We never want to be the first to guess the top or bottom in a strong trend. We get the counter trend play once we get the structure shift AND confirmed selling at the structure level."

**Two conditions. Both required. Not one.**
1. Structure shift — key shelf lost
2. Confirmed selling at that level — lower high retest + aggressive prints

---

## The 6-Step Model (SHORT example)

### Step 01 — DISTRIBUTION (WATCH ONLY)
- Heavy two-way orderflow at the highs
- Stacked green AND red prints = buyers and sellers fighting
- No clear winner yet
- **DO NOT ENTER. Just watch.**
- This is not the top call — it's information gathering

### Step 02 — STRUCTURE BREAK (FIRST TELL)
- Key shelf level breaks (price closes below it)
- "First real tell — supply takes control"
- Structure has shifted but NOT confirmed yet
- **STILL NO ENTRY. Wait for confirmation.**

### Step 03 — LOWER HIGH RETEST (ENTRY)
- Price bounces back UP to the broken shelf from below
- Makes a LOWER HIGH (doesn't reach the original high)
- Aggressive sell prints fire at the retest (big red bubble)
- Sellers defending the broken level
- **THIS IS THE ENTRY. Short here.**
- Entry: at the lower high retest with sell confirmation
- Stop: above the lower high

### Step 04 — THE TRAP
- Price stalls near the next major level below (OH/key support)
- Early shorts get squeezed briefly
- Buying steps in temporarily above the level
- Those trapped shorts = fuel for the flush when the level breaks

### Step 05 — REJECTION
- Final bounce attempt at the support level
- Sellers absorb the bid
- Trapped longs get stopped out
- Clears the path for the flush

### Step 06 — FLUSH
- Key level gives way
- Stop cascade
- Clean drive to target

## Entry Checklist — Counter Trend Short

- [ ] Strong uptrend visible — don't fade blindly
- [ ] Distribution at highs — two-way flow, not clean buying
- [ ] Key shelf level identified (consolidation zone, HVN edge, value area)
- [ ] Shelf breaks — price closes below it (Step 02)
- [ ] Price bounces back to broken shelf — lower high forms (Step 03)
- [ ] Aggressive sell prints at the retest (red bubble + negative delta)
- [ ] SHORT entry on the lower high retest with confirmation
- [ ] Stop above the lower high
- [ ] Target = next structural level below (OH, VAL, next HVN)

---

## Entry Checklist — Counter Trend Long (Mirror)

- [ ] Strong downtrend visible
- [ ] Absorption at lows — two-way flow
- [ ] Key shelf level identified
- [ ] Shelf breaks upward — price closes above
- [ ] Price pulls back to broken shelf — higher low forms
- [ ] Aggressive buy prints at the retest (green bubble + positive delta)
- [ ] LONG entry on the higher low retest with confirmation
- [ ] Stop below the higher low
- [ ] Target = next structural level above

---

## The Key Distinction

| Moment | Action | Why |
|--------|--------|-----|
| Two-way flow at extreme | WATCH | Too risky, no clear winner |
| Shelf breaks | FIRST TELL | Still needs confirmation |
| Lower high retest + sell prints | **ENTRY** | Structure shift confirmed |

## What This Is NOT

- NOT shorting the actual top (two-way flow = too risky)
- NOT shorting when the shelf breaks (needs confirmation)
- NOT a random fade of a strong trend
- NOT based on one bubble alone — needs structure break + retest + prints

---

## The One-Line Summary

> Wait for the shelf to break. Wait for price to come back and fail at that shelf. When sellers confirm at the retest — that's your entry. Not before.
