## LAF — Look Above & Fail (SHORT)
**Live example from Yush | VAH 27,910**

### The 3-Step Sequence

**Step 1 — Sweep: Buyers Aggressive**
- Price breaks ABOVE the level (VAH 27,910)
- Big green bubble fires (79 contracts) = aggressive buyers chasing the breakout
- They think it's going higher — they're now committed above the level
- The trap is being SET

**Step 2 — Absorption**
- Red bubble fires at the level (76 contracts) = sellers absorbing every buy
- Heavy sell delta (162 bubble) confirms = massive sell pressure eating buyers
- Price CANNOT hold above VAH despite all the buying
- The buyers got their fills. They did not get the move.

**Step 3 — Lose VAH → Entry**
- Price closes BACK BELOW VAH 27,910
- Breakout confirmed fake
- Buyers trapped above 27,910 — forced to sell
- Their stops become sell orders = fuel for the short
- **SHORT entry on the close back below the level**

**Stop:** Above the sweep high
**Result:** -65 points (27,910 → 27,845)

### The One-Line Summary
> Price sweeps VAH → buyers chase → sellers absorb → price loses VAH → buyers trapped → SHORT

## LBF — Look Below & Fail (LONG)
*Mirror image of LAF*

### The 3-Step Sequence

**Step 1 — Sweep: Sellers Aggressive**
- Price breaks BELOW the level (VAL / support)
- Big red bubble fires = aggressive sellers chasing the breakdown
- They think it's going lower — they're now committed below the level
- The trap is being SET

**Step 2 — Absorption**
- Green bubble fires at the level = buyers absorbing every sell
- Heavy buy delta confirms = buyers eating all the selling
- Price CANNOT hold below the level despite all the selling
- The sellers got their fills. They did not get the move.

**Step 3 — Reclaim → Entry**
- Price closes BACK ABOVE the level
- Breakdown confirmed fake
- Sellers trapped below — forced to buy (cover shorts)
- Their stops become buy orders = fuel for the long
- **LONG entry on the close back above the level**

**Stop:** Below the sweep low
**Result:** Move up to next structural level

### The One-Line Summary
> Price sweeps VAL → sellers chase → buyers absorb → price reclaims VAL → sellers trapped → LONG

## The Critical Rules for Both Setups

### Rule 1 — Location determines the trade
| Signal Location | Setup | Direction |
|----------------|-------|-----------|
| Green bubbles at HIGH / resistance | LAF | SHORT |
| Green bubbles at LOW after flush | LBF | LONG |
| Red bubbles at HIGH | LAF | SHORT |
| Red bubbles at LOW / support | LBF | LONG |

**This is the #1 mistake Sandy has made. Same signal, wrong location = wrong trade.**

### Rule 2 — The level must be real
Valid levels for LAF/LBF:
- VAH / VAL
- OH / OL (Opening High / Low)
- PL (Previous Level)
- Range High / Range Low
- Multi-day HVN
- Session High / Low

NOT valid:
- Random mid-range price
- No level nearby
- Guessing

### Rule 3 — Three confirmations required
- [ ] Price swept BEYOND the level (not just touched it — a real move through)
- [ ] Aggressive flow in the sweep direction (big bubble + matching delta)
- [ ] Price CLOSES back through the level (not a wick — a candle body close)

### Rule 4 — Entry is on the CLOSE back through
Not on the wick. Not anticipating. When the candle CLOSES back through the level → that's the entry.

### Rule 5 — Invalidation
- For LAF short: another big green bubble retests AND price closes back above the level → exit, thesis wrong
- For LBF long: another big red bubble retests AND price closes back below the level → exit, thesis wrong

## Trade Review Checklist
*Use this for every trade Sandy submits*

### Before entry — ask these:
1. **What is the level?** (Name it — VAH, OL, PL, etc.)
2. **Did price genuinely sweep through it?** (Body move, not just a wick)
3. **What was the aggressive flow?** (Which bubble, how big, which direction)
4. **Did the delta confirm?** (Heavy delta matching the bubble color)
5. **Did price CLOSE back through the level?** (Entry trigger)
6. **What day type is this?** (Trend or balance)
7. **Is the signal at a HIGH or a LOW?** (Determines direction)
8. **Where is the stop?** (Beyond the sweep extreme)
9. **What is the target?** (Next structural level)

### Grading system:
- **A+** — All 9 boxes correct, trade worked
- **A** — All 9 boxes correct, trade stopped (market beat you, not your fault)
- **B** — 7-8 boxes correct, small mistake in execution
- **C** — 5-6 boxes, significant error in setup identification
- **F** — Wrong direction, wrong level, or no confirmation

## LBF Live Example — Yush | VAL + OL 7410

**Setup:** LBF · Look Below & Fail | Long · reclaim of OL & POI on absorption

### The Level
VAL · OL **7410** — double confluence (two levels stacking = stronger setup)

### Step 1 — Sweep: Sellers Aggressive
- Price swept DOWN to L: 7399 (11 pts below the level)
- **320 red bubble** + **249 red bubble** = massive aggressive selling
- Sellers fully committed below 7410

### Step 2 — Absorption
- *"heavy bid · no reaction down"*
- Green bubble at the low = passive buyer absorbing everything
- 320 + 249 contracts of selling → price went nowhere
- Sellers got their fills. They got nothing else.

### Step 3 — Reclaim → Entry
- *"aggressive buyers · reclaim OL & POI"*
- Green bubble fires at 7410
- Price closes BACK ABOVE VAL/OL 7410
- Sellers trapped below → forced to cover → fuel for the long

**Entry:** Reclaim close above 7410
**Stop:** 7408 zone (2-3 pts)
**Target:** VAH 7420
**Result:** +10 pts ✅
**R:R:** 1:4

### Key lessons from this example
1. **Double confluence** (VAL + OL at same price) = higher conviction
2. **Sweep size doesn't matter** — absorption does. 11 pts below but no reaction = sellers trapped
3. **R:R was 1:4** — wait for right setup at right level, not every signal
4. **Target = next level** — entered at VAL/OL, target was VAH. Level to level.
