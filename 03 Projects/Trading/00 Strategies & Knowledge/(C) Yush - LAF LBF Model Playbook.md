# (C) Yush — LAF / LBF Model Playbook
*Volume Profile · Order Flow · Failed Liquidity Sweeps*
**Motto: TRADE THE TRAP**

---

## Core Logic

Price sweeps beyond the Value Area boundary (VAH or VAL) with **aggressive flow** — but then **fails to extend**. The flow is absorbed. Price reclaims back inside the value area. This is a trap — the aggressive traders are now offside. Enter when the reclaim candle closes back through the boundary.

---

## The Two Setups

### LBF — Look Below & Fail (LONG)

**What happens:**
1. Volume Profile drawn with VAH, VAL, POC clearly marked
2. Price sweeps **below VAL** — not just touches it, actually goes through
3. Aggressive sell prints (red bubbles) are visible — heavy sell flow below VAL
4. Despite that sell flow, price **stops extending lower** — absorption is happening
5. A candle **closes back above VAL** — the reclaim

**Entry:** Reclaim candle close back above VAL
**Stop:** Below the sweep low
**Target 1:** POC
**Target 2:** VAH

---

### LAF — Look Above & Fail (SHORT)

**What happens:**
1. Volume Profile drawn with VAH, VAL, POC clearly marked
2. Price sweeps **above VAH** — not just touches it, actually goes through
3. Aggressive buy prints (green bubbles) are visible — heavy buy flow above VAH
4. Despite that buy flow, price **stops extending higher** — absorption is happening
5. A candle **closes back below VAH** — the loss of VAH

**Entry:** Candle close back below VAH — the rejection confirmed
**Stop:** Above the sweep high
**Target 1:** POC
**Target 2:** VAL

## Pre-Trade Checklist — The Five Boxes
*Tick all 5 or pass*

- [ ] **01** — Volume Profile is drawn with VAH, VAL, and POC clearly marked
- [ ] **02** — Price has **swept** beyond VAH (LAF) or VAL (LBF) — not just touched it
- [ ] **03** — Aggressive flow in the direction of the sweep is visible on delta or footprint (red bubbles below VAL / green bubbles above VAH)
- [ ] **04** — Price has **stopped extending** despite that aggressive flow — absorption is on the tape
- [ ] **05** — A reclaim candle has **closed** back through VAH (LAF) or VAL (LBF)

---

## Key Concepts

**VAH (Value Area High):** Upper boundary of where 70% of volume traded — institutional reference
**VAL (Value Area Low):** Lower boundary of value area — same significance
**POC (Point of Control):** Single price with highest volume — strongest magnet
**Sweep:** Price genuinely goes beyond the level — a body move, not just a wick tap
**Absorption:** Aggressive flow prints but price refuses to follow through = institutions eating the trap traders
**Reclaim:** A candle body closes back through the level — confirms the trap is complete

---

## LBF vs LAF — Quick Reference

| | LBF (Long) | LAF (Short) |
|--|--|--|
| Sweep direction | Below VAL | Above VAH |
| Aggressive flow | Red bubbles (sellers) | Green bubbles (buyers) |
| Absorption tells | Sell volume spikes but price halts | Buy volume spikes but price halts |
| Entry trigger | Reclaim close above VAL | Loss close below VAH |
| Stop | Below sweep low | Above sweep high |
| Target 1 | POC | POC |
| Target 2 | VAH | VAL |

---

## What Makes This an A+ Setup

- Sweep is clean and impulsive — price went clearly through the level
- Aggressive flow is heavy and one-sided (lots of red/green bubbles)
- Absorption is obvious — big prints, price doesn't move further
- Reclaim candle is strong — closes well back through the level
- POC is clearly defined and acts as a natural magnet target

---

## What to Avoid

- Wick-only tap of VAH/VAL without a real sweep → skip
- Weak, mixed flow on the sweep (no clear aggressive prints) → skip  
- Slow grind back through the level (not a reclaim candle) → skip
- POC too close to entry — poor R:R → skip
- Chasing the entry after the reclaim candle has already moved far → skip

## Where This Setup Triggers — All Valid Locations

LAF/LBF is not limited to session VAH/VAL. It works at **any significant range boundary**:

| Location | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Session VAH / VAL | Primary — most reliable |
| **Range High / Range Low** | Consolidation box extremes — very common |
| Weekly VAH / WVAH | Higher timeframe = stronger sweep |
| Weekly VAL / WVAL | Higher timeframe = stronger sweep |
| Previous Day High / Low | PDH / PDL acting as range boundary |
| Opening Range High / Low | ORH / ORL — first 30 min range extremes |

**The level type doesn't change the setup. The mechanics are the same.** Sweep → aggressive flow → absorption → reclaim.

---

## Confluence Factors — What Makes It Stronger

Yush looks for **additional confluence** at the sweep zone. The more that stack, the higher the conviction:

- **WVAH / WVAL** — weekly value area aligning with the sweep level
- **WLVN** — weekly low volume node at or near the level (price will move fast through)
- **PD Rays** — prior day developing levels acting as confluence
- **Intersection areas** — multiple levels converging at the same price zone
- **Resistance turned support** — a level that was previous resistance, broke above, now retested as support → buy the retest

> *The more that stack at one price, the stronger the trap.*

---

## Entry Technique — Scaling In

Yush doesn't always enter full size at once. He scales in:

1. **First absorption signal** → small entry (e.g. L3 = 3 contracts)
2. **Confirmation of hold + more absorption** → add size (e.g. L10 = 10 contracts)
3. Stop sits below the sweep low for the full position

This means if the first entry gets hit, the stop covers both. If it goes in your favour, the average entry is better.

---

## Resistance Turned Support (Standalone Concept)

**The setup:**
- Price was previously capped at a level (resistance)
- Price breaks **above** that level with conviction
- Price pulls back to retest that old resistance level
- Old resistance **becomes support** — buyers defend it
- Footprint shows absorption at that level on the retest

**Entry:** On the retest when absorption confirms
**Stop:** Below the support level
**Target:** Next resistance / HVN above

This is NOT a LAF/LBF — it's a clean trend continuation. Use it when the overall bias is bullish and you're looking for a re-entry after a breakout.
