# (C) Yush — Premarket Process

---

## The Goal of Premarket

Define **1-2 specific price zones** to watch at open. Not a thesis. Not a prediction. A plan with clear levels and clear conditions.

*"Stay patient — let price come to us."*

---

## What He Marks Every Morning

### 1. Overnight Levels
- **OVH** — Overnight High (Buy Side Liquidity resting above)
- **OVL** — Overnight Low (Sell Side Liquidity resting below)
- *"watching ES and NQ to sweep OVL for a long scalp"*
- *"watch how it reacts on OVL retest"*

### 2. Volume Profile Levels (Previous RTH Session)
- **VAH** — Value Area High
- **VAL** — Value Area Low
- **POC** — Point of Control (midpoint target for most trades)
- Looks for: current price vs yesterday's value area — inside or outside?
- *"notice where we created value in NY yesterday? VAH. can buyers defend it?"*

### 3. Low Volume Nodes (LVN Pockets)
- Identifies thin areas on the profile where price will move fast
- *"overnight we created Low volume pocket, can sellers defend $25,500 or do we start filling in the LVN?"*
- LVN fills = directional moves coming
- *"watching how we act inside the LVN tomorrow if we get a retest"*

### 4. Double Distribution Profiles
- Two separate value areas visible on the profile = LVN bridge between them
- *"notice how the volume cluster is building out for the double D profile?"*
- *"have the two ranges marked on the chart, lets see where we open tomorrow"*
- The LVN between the distributions is the speed zone for the day's move

### 5. Key Structure Levels
- Previous Day High (PDH) / Previous Day Low (PDL)
- Opening Range High (ORH) / Opening Range Low (ORL) — first 30 min range
- Weekly levels (WVAH, WVAL) — when relevant
- *"this is the same zone where we built a volume cluster yesterday before the move down"*

---

## How He Frames the Plan

### The "Watch" Format
Every level comes with a condition:

*"watching X level — can [buyers/sellers] defend OR is this a failed [breakout/breakdown]?"*

Examples from Discord:
- *"6505-6510 — start of the impulsive launch on news Monday. Now below — can sellers hold as resistance or is this a failed breakout where we reclaim and launch higher?"*
- *"I'd watch 6961-6963 on ES if we trade there today"*
- *"watching how price acts once we take out Overnight high"*

### The "Scenario" Format
He pre-defines what he needs to see:

*"If X happens → I'm watching for Y"*

Examples:
- *"if we don't break out of this range in NY no live stream today cuz I have no interest in trading it"*
- *"if those don't get tested no trade for me until post news 10 am est"*
- *"7310-7305 is the big level if we test it today, notice the volume build up down there"*

---

## Day Type Assessment

Before defining levels, he reads the **day type** from the developing profile:

**If overnight was choppy in same range as RTH:**
→ *"if we don't break out of this range in NY no live stream today"* — balance day expected, minimal interest

**If overnight created an impulsive move:**
→ Identifies the LVN created by that move as the key zone
→ *"overnight we just chopped in the same range as RTH"* = balance → fade extremes

**If double distribution developing:**
→ Marks both value areas, identifies the LVN between them as the speed zone
→ Watches for price to enter the LVN → fast move to the next cluster

**If strong trend overnight:**
→ *"strong move to new ATH, I wanna see how we react there if we get a retest today"*

## Post-Premarket: Open Reaction Read

When the open arrives, Yush watches how price **reacts** at his pre-marked levels before entering:

1. Does price test the level?
2. Is there orderflow at the level? (Big prints, passive buyer/seller visible)
3. Is price accepting above/below or rejecting?
4. Is it a sweep + reclaim (LBF/LAF) or a clean break?

If the level isn't tested by 9:55 AM → he voids the idea and waits for post-news

*"voiding this if it doesnt get tested by 9:55 am est (too close to the news)"*

---

## When He Chooses NOT to Trade

- Level never gets tested
- ES and NQ moving in opposite directions
- Open is pure range-bound chop with no structure
- Within 2 minutes of news
- Spread is too wide (NQ spread 2.5-3 points = no touch)
- Already hit daily risk limit
- Not feeling right after a bad previous session

*"just watching today"* — he says this often and means it. Cash is a position.
