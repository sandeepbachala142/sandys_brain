## Example 01 — Resistance Turned Support (Live Chart)

**Setup type:** Resistance turned support
**Platform:** MotiveWave (with footprint)

**What happened:**
- Orange horizontal line = prior resistance level (POC or VAH from previous session)
- Price broke above it, then pulled back to retest
- Green bubble appeared at the retest = buyer absorption confirmed
- Level held as support → price continued higher

**Key tell:** Green absorption bubble RIGHT at the orange line on the retest. That's the signal — not the level alone, but the footprint confirming defense.

**Lesson:** Mark your prior session VAH/POC levels. When price breaks above and comes back → watch for the green bubble. That's your long.

## Example 03 — Live Trade: LBF at Range Low with Scale-In

**Setup type:** LBF at Range Low
**Platform:** MotiveWave (footprint + VP)

**Chart details:**
- Black rounded rectangle = defined range/balance area
- ORH: 25,663.75 (Opening Range High)
- Range roughly 25,620 — 25,635
- Orange dashed lines = VWAP / session levels
- Pink dashed lines = weekly levels / PD rays

**What happened:**
- Price sitting in balance zone, making equal lows at ~25,620 (range low)
- Green bubble "L3" appeared at range low → first entry (3 contracts)
- More absorption confirmed → added "L10" (10 contracts) at same area
- Footprint on right: heavy negative delta (-177, -191, -186) = sellers active
- BUT price not extending lower despite sellers → absorption happening
- Another green bubble "5" below the range = confirming the sweep low was absorbed

**Entry technique:**
1. First green bubble at range low → L3 (small starter)
2. Absorption holds → added L10 (scale in)
3. Stop below the sweep low

**Key tell:** Heavy negative delta in the footprint BUT price not moving lower. When sellers are pushing hard and price doesn't follow → that's the tell. Buyers are eating every sell order.

**Lesson:** Don't just look at the green bubbles. Look at the DELTA. If delta is heavily negative but price is flat or rising → absorption is happening → long setup.

## Pattern Recognition Notes

From all examples so far, Yush's entries share these common fingerprints:

1. **A clearly defined level** — not guesswork, an actual VP or range boundary
2. **A sweep through it** — price went beyond, not just touched
3. **Aggressive flow in sweep direction** — footprint shows the trap being set
4. **Absorption** — flow doesn't produce price extension = institutions absorbing
5. **Reclaim or defense** — price comes back through the level aggressively
6. **Confluence stacking** — weekly levels / PD rays / range extremes all pointing same way

The more of these boxes that tick → the bigger the size.
