# (C) Yush — LVN Model Playbook
*Volume Cluster · Edge Break · LVN Defense*
**Motto: DEFEND THE EDGE**

---

## Core Logic

A High Volume Node (HVN) cluster acts as a base. Price breaks out of the cluster into the thin Low Volume Node (LVN) above or below. On the pullback, if price defends **well before** re-entering the cluster → **the breakout is real → enter in the direction of the break.**

The LVN is thin = price moves fast through it. Your target is the other end of the LVN, then the next HVN beyond.

---

## Setup 01 — LONG (Breakout / LVN Defended by Buyers)

**What happens:**
1. HVN cluster forms — clear upper and lower edges visible on profile
2. Price breaks **above** the upper cluster edge — body close beyond it (not a wick)
3. Price pulls back into the LVN — but **shallowly** (stalls well before the cluster edge)
4. Big green prints fire at the retest — buyer absorption on footprint
5. Delta confirms one-sided buying — sellers being eaten, price refuses to re-enter cluster

**Entry:** When price defends above the cluster edge and absorption confirms
**Stop:** Just inside the cluster (below the broken upper edge)
**Target 1:** Opposite (upper) end of the LVN
**Target 2:** Next HVN above

---

## Setup 02 — SHORT (Breakdown / LVN Defended by Sellers)

**What happens:**
1. HVN cluster forms — clear upper and lower edges visible on profile
2. Price breaks **below** the lower cluster edge — body close beyond it (not a wick)
3. Price pulls back into the LVN — but **shallowly** (stalls well before the cluster edge)
4. Big red prints fire at the retest — seller absorption on footprint
5. Delta confirms one-sided selling — buyers being eaten, price refuses to re-enter cluster

**Entry:** When price defends below the cluster edge and absorption confirms
**Stop:** Just inside the cluster (above the broken lower edge)
**Target 1:** Opposite (lower) end of the LVN
**Target 2:** Next HVN below

## Pre-Trade Checklist — The Five Boxes
*Tick all 5 or pass*

- [ ] **01** — HVN cluster is mapped. Both upper and lower edges are clearly defined on the profile
- [ ] **02** — Price has broken cleanly through one cluster edge into the adjacent LVN — a **body close** beyond the edge, not a wick poke
- [ ] **03** — Price retraces back into the LVN — but **shallowly**. Pullback stalls well before the cluster edge, leaving most of the LVN unfilled
- [ ] **04** — Big trade prints fire at the retest in the defense direction — green bubbles (long) or red bubbles (short)
- [ ] **05** — Delta shows **one-sided absorption** — opposing flow is eaten, price refuses to re-enter the cluster

---

## Key Concepts

**HVN (High Volume Node):** Area of heavy trading activity — acts as a magnet and support/resistance
**LVN (Low Volume Node):** Thin area with little historical volume — price moves fast through it
**Cluster edge:** The upper or lower boundary of the HVN — the line price must break and defend
**Shallow pullback:** The retest only fills the outer portion of the LVN — NOT back to the cluster edge
**Absorption:** Big prints hit but price doesn't move. Institutions eating the opposing flow.

---

## What Makes This an A+ Setup

- Pullback is very shallow — barely enters the LVN before reversing
- Absorption fires immediately on the defense candle
- Delta is clean and one-sided — no back and forth
- Volume cluster is well-defined (not a messy, diffuse HVN)
- LVN above/below is clearly thin — fast move expected once defended

---

## What to Avoid

- Wick poke through the cluster edge (not a body close) → skip
- Deep pullback that fills most of the LVN → cluster edge at risk → skip
- Choppy, mixed delta at the retest → no clear absorption → skip
- Messy, undefined cluster edges → can't identify the line to defend → skip
