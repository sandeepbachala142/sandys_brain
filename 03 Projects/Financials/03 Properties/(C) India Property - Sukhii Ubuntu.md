## RERA Claim

### What You're Entitled To
Under **Telangana RERA**, since the builder missed the possession date + grace period:
- **Interest on amount paid** at SBI MCLR + 2% per annum, for every month of delay
- Calculated on the total amount paid to the builder
- Runs from the end of the grace period (June 2026) until actual possession

### Builder's Escape Hatch
The force majeure clause (war, flood, drought, fire, cyclone, earthquake) is the builder's only defense. They must prove a genuine declared calamity — normal construction delays don't qualify.

### How to File
- File complaint at: **https://rera.telangana.gov.in**
- Forum: Telangana Real Estate Regulatory Authority
- Required: Agreement copy, payment receipts, proof of non-possession
