## Sleep

Trading NQ means being active at night (US pre-market ~8:30 PM IST). Sleep 
has to be protected despite late nights.

**Target:** 7–8 hours. Non-negotiable.

**Rules:**
- No phone in bed
- Wind down starts at 11 PM — dim lights, no trading analysis
- If I miss sleep, I do NOT trade the next session. Sleep deprivation + trading = 
  stupid decisions with real money

## Mental Health

The honest version:

**Stressors right now:**
- ~5 months of financial runway — this is the big one
- New city (Hyderabad), new routine, everything in flux
- Trading not profitable yet — pressure building

**What actually helps (not theory, actual experience):**
- Morning walk — clears the head better than anything
- Talking to wife — she grounds me
- Making something (scripting, recording) — creative work as therapy
- Singing — even 10 minutes alone

**What doesn't help but I do anyway:**
- Doom-scrolling when stressed
- Overanalyzing trades after the fact
- Comparing my growth to other creators

**When I notice I'm not okay:** I default to movies, food, walks as avoidance 
rather than reset. Know the difference. If it's the third movie in a row, it's 
probably avoidance.

## Metrics I Actually Care About

Not weight. Not step counts. Just these:

- Am I sleeping 7+ hours most nights?
- Did I move today?
- Do I have energy by afternoon?
- Am I stress-eating less than last week?

That's it. If those are yes, I'm doing fine.
