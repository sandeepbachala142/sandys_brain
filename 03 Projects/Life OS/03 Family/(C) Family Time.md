# Family Time

> Everything I'm building is for them. But "for them" has to include *with them*. 
> This is where I think about how to actually show up.

---

## My Family

**Wife** — partner in all of this. She moved with me, supported the move back to India, 
manages the household while I'm figuring out income. She doesn't get enough credit.

**Son** — 4 years old. Absolute force of nature. This age goes fast. He's not going 
to remember that I was "working on something big" — he's going to remember if I was 
there or not.

**Parents** — in India, which is part of why coming back made sense. Want to be 
closer and more present for them.

---

## How I Want to Show Up

### With my son:
- **1 hour of real time every day** — not me on my phone in the same room. Actual 
  play, reading, talking, whatever he wants.
- Weekend mornings are his — no trading, no content stress before 10 AM on weekends
- Take him somewhere new at least once a month — park, activity, trip, anything
- Be the dad who shows up to things

### With my wife:
- Talk — actually talk, not just logistics about the day
- Date nights: at least once a month, just the two of us
- Make sure she knows the plan — she's my partner in this, not just a bystander
- Don't bring trading stress to dinner

### With parents:
- Call at least 2x a week
- Visit when I can — now that I'm in India, this is possible
- Help without waiting to be asked

---

## The Tension I Need to Manage

Trading at night + content work in the day = I'm "home" all day but not always 
*present*. That's the trap.

**Signs I'm doing it wrong:**
- Son keeps asking me to play and I keep saying "5 more minutes"
- Wife and I are only talking about logistics, not actually connecting
- I'm on my phone during dinner
- Last meaningful activity with son was "can't remember"

**Signs I'm doing it right:**
- Son talks about what we did together
- Wife and I had a real conversation today
- I was genuinely present for at least 1 hour today — not performing presence

- [ ] Get settled in Hyderabad — stable home, stable routine
- [ ] Take a family trip — even just a 2-3 day thing once we're settled
- [ ] Son in a school he loves
- [ ] Wife has space to do things she wants to do, not just manage the house
- [ ] Visit wife's parents at least once this year
- [ ] Weekend tradition with son — something we do every week, just us

---

## Monthly Family Check-In

> Ask myself these on the 1st of each month.

- Did I have real time with my son this month (not just presence)?
- Did my wife and I connect, or just coordinate?
- Did I call my parents enough?
- Am I building this life *with* them or just telling them about it later?
