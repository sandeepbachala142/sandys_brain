# Life Vision

> Not goals. Not KPIs. What I actually want my life to look like.

---

## The Big Picture

I want a life where:

- I wake up and **choose** what I do that day — not because a boss said so
- My **family is taken care of** — financially, emotionally, time-wise
- I'm creating things that **connect with real people** at scale
- Trading is **reliable income** that gives me freedom, not stress
- I have **time and energy** for the things that make me feel alive — singing, 
  sports, travel, time with people I love
- I'm **proud of how I showed up** — not just what I achieved

That's it. That's the whole thing.

---

## What Financial Freedom Means to Me

Not lamborghinis. Not "passive income" fantasy. Specifically:

- **Pay my family's bills comfortably** — no stress about rent, school fees, basics
- **Take 2 trips a year** — real ones, not rushed
- **Give my parents whatever they need** — no hesitation
- **Work because I want to** — not because I have to
- **Not think about money** for small decisions — coffee, auto, dinner out

Target income: **₹5–6 lakh/month** would genuinely change everything.

---

## 5-Year Picture (2031)

If things go the way I want:

- Trading is consistent — $10k+/month, maybe more
- YouTube is real — 500k+ subscribers, actual community
- Living in Hyderabad in a home we love
- Son is in school, thriving, knows his dad shows up
- Have done at least 5 trips across India + maybe 1 abroad
- Singing is still just for me — and that's fine

## What I'm NOT Chasing

Equally important. Things I'm consciously opting out of:

- Fame for fame's sake — reach is a means, not the goal
- The hustle-culture grind that costs me my family and health
- Validation from people who don't know me
- Building content around a persona that isn't me
- "Scaling" trading into something that stresses me out more than it's worth

---

## Core Values

The things I won't compromise:

1. **Family first** — my wife and son are not a cost of success, they're the reason for it
2. **Honesty** — in content, in trading, in how I talk about my life
3. **Not making people feel bad** — hard line. Not a strategy I'll use.
4. **Doing things my way** — I'd rather be slow and authentic than fast and fake
