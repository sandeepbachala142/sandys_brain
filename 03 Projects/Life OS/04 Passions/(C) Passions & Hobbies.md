## Singing 🎤

Not professional. Not chasing it. Just something that's deeply in me.

**What it is for me:** Pure joy. No audience needed. I sing because it feels 
good, not because it's useful or performative.

**How I want to keep this:**
- Sing when I want to — in the car, at home, whenever
- Don't let it become a "content thing" unless it naturally wants to be
- Smule sessions with the friends gang — that's a real thing worth keeping

**A thought:** The one time I might share it publicly is if it's a genuine 
expression of something, not a strategy. The Friends Gang Smule story would 
actually make a great Reel — but only because it's a real story.

## Travel ✈️

Not where I am right now financially, but it's on the list and I want to 
keep it alive in my mind so I actually do it when I can.

**Philosophy:** Don't wait for the "right time." Budget trips count. 
2-day road trips count. Getting out counts.

**India priority list:**
1. Meghalaya / Northeast — nature, different culture, genuinely haven't been
2. Ladakh / Spiti — always been on the list
3. South India road trip — temple trails, coast, I barely know my own backyard

**International (when the time is right):**
1. Japan — food, culture, order, everything I'd love
2. USA trip with son — show him where he was born and spent his early years

**Travel rule:** At least 2 trips a year once income is stable. Even short ones.

## Sports in General

I love watching sports. It's my downtime. I'm not apologizing for it.

- Cricket is life. Always.
- Formula 1 — got into it, enjoy it
- Badminton at the major events
- Football occasionally

This isn't wasted time. It's the thing I do to recharge. The key is not 
letting it become 6 hours of avoidance — 2 hours of cricket while relaxing 
is fine, 6 hours of T20 marathons to avoid difficult things is not.

## Protecting These Things

The biggest risk: these get cut first when life gets busy.

**The rule:** At least one passion activity per week. If a week goes by with 
zero — badminton, singing, sport I enjoyed watching — I'm running on empty 
and I should check what's going on.
