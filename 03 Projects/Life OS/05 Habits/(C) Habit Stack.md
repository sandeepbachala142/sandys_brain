## Habits to Kill

Things I do that I know aren't helping:

| Habit | Why it's a problem | What to replace it with |
|-------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Phone in bed | Kills sleep quality, starts the day reactive | Charge phone outside the room |
| Stress eating at night | Coping mechanism, not actual help | Identify the real stressor and address it |
| "5 more minutes" with son | It's always more than 5. He notices. | Set a hard stop and actually stop |
| Overanalyzing closed trades | Decision is made. Nothing changes. | Log it and move on |
| Doom scrolling when stressed | Makes it worse, not better | Walk. Even 10 minutes. |

---

## How to Build a Habit (My Rule)

Don't add more than **2 new habits at a time**. I've tried the big reset before. 
It doesn't stick.

**Process:**
1. Pick 1-2 habits from the stack above
2. Do them for 3 weeks without worrying about anything else
3. Once they're automatic, add the next one
4. If I break the streak, start over — no drama, no guilt
