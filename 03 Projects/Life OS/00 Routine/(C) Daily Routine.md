# Daily Routine

> The architecture of my day. Not a rigid schedule — a default I return to.

**Status:** Draft — needs to be tested and adjusted

---

## The Ideal Day (Draft)

This is built around NQ market hours (US pre-market starts ~8:30 PM IST) and 
the reality of having a 4-year-old at home.

### Morning Block — Build & Think
| Time | What |
|------|------|
| 6:30 AM | Wake up. No phone for first 15 min. |
| 6:45 AM | Morning walk or light movement — get the body going |
| 7:15 AM | Tea, breakfast, morning time with family |
| 8:00 AM | Content work OR personal study (trading research, skill building) |
| 10:00 AM | Focused content creation — scripting, filming, editing |

### Afternoon Block — Family & Recharge
| Time | What |
|------|------|
| 12:30 PM | Lunch + downtime |
| 1:30 PM | Family time / son's activities / errands |
| 3:30 PM | Rest or light creative work (singing, reading, walk) |
| 5:00 PM | Pre-market prep for trading — review levels, bias, setup watchlist |

### Evening / Night Block — Trade
| Time | What |
|------|------|
| 7:00 PM | Dinner + family wind-down |
| 8:00 PM | Pre-market analysis finalized |
| 8:30 PM | Markets open — trade NQ if setup is valid |
| 10:30 PM | Post-trade review — log the trade, quick notes |
| 11:00 PM | Wind down. No screens after this ideally. |
| 11:30 PM | Sleep |

## Weekly Rhythm

| Day | Focus |
|-----|-------|
| Monday | Plan the week — trading + content calendar |
| Tuesday | Content creation day |
| Wednesday | Content creation day |
| Thursday | Review trades so far, refine bias |
| Friday | Light day — admin, family, recharge |
| Saturday | Family day — mostly offline |
| Sunday | Weekly review + prep for the week ahead |

---

## Non-Negotiables

These happen every day, no matter what:

- [ ] Morning movement (walk, stretch — anything)
- [ ] 1 hour of meaningful time with my son
- [ ] Trade review if I traded that day
- [ ] 8 hours of sleep

---

## What to Protect

The things that get eaten first when life gets busy — don't let them:

- **Morning walk** — my mental reset. Skip this and the day goes sideways.
- **Son time** — he's 4. These years don't come back.
- **Sleep** — trading without sleep is gambling.
- **Weekly review** — without it I lose the thread of what's working.
